# Exercise 1: Set Up Gemini as Your First Staff Member

:::key-concept
You'll set up the Gemini app with a business profile, connect only the apps you need, and run one real task on two models to see the difference. At the end you'll have a saved "Business Profile" Google Doc you'll reuse in every exercise of this course.
:::

**Time:** 25 minutes · **You'll need:** A Google account (Google AI Pro recommended, free works with limits), access to gemini.google.com · **Builds on:** Lesson 1

## Warm-up

:::checkpoint
Q: Which model should you start with for a routine job like rewriting a customer email?
- [ ] Gemini 4 Argon, always, because it's the best
- [x] Flash, then move up only if the answer is weak
- [ ] It doesn't matter; all models give the same answer
Why: Flash handles everyday work well and uses less of your allowance. Save Pro and Argon for hard or high-stakes work.
:::

## Steps

1. **Pick your business.** If you don't have an idea yet, use the sample business for this course: "Groomer Books," monthly bookkeeping for independent dog groomers in the US.
2. **Check your plan.** In the Gemini app, open the model menu by the prompt box and write down which models you see. Compare with the [Google AI plans page](https://gemini.google/subscriptions/). Note whether you have Pro and Gemini 4 Argon.
3. **Draft your business profile.** Start a new chat on Flash and paste:

```text
Interview me to write a business profile for my AI assistant.
Ask one question at a time, eight questions total, covering: what I sell, who buys, their main problem, my prices or price range, my tone of voice, what I never do, my tools, and my goal for the next 90 days.
After the last answer, write a profile under 200 words in second person ("You are working for...").
```

4. **Save it.** Copy the profile into a new Google Doc named "Business Profile." This is your source of truth.
5. **Add it to Customize.** Open Customize in the left menu of the Gemini app and add the profile so Gemini uses it in every chat. If you don't see Customize yet, keep the Doc and attach it to chats for now.
6. **Connect apps on purpose.** In the same area, connect Gmail and Drive only if you'll use them in this course. Write one line in your Doc listing what you connected and why.
7. **Run a model test.** Paste the same prompt into a Flash chat and a Pro (or Argon) chat:

```text
Using my business profile, write the three questions a skeptical first-time customer would ask before buying, and a short, honest answer to each.
```

8. **Compare.** In your Doc, add a short table: model, what it did well, what it got wrong, which you'd use for this job.

## Done when

- [ ] You have a "Business Profile" Google Doc under 200 words
- [ ] The profile is in Customize, or you've noted it isn't available yet on your account
- [ ] You've listed which apps you connected and why
- [ ] You've compared two models on the same prompt and picked a default

:::coached
**For 1-on-1 coaching clients.** Do this on your real business, then push it further:

1. Write a second profile for your ideal customer ("You are a groomer who...") and save it as its own Doc.
2. Run the customer-question prompt on three models and score each answer 1 to 5 on accuracy and usefulness.
3. Draft a one-page "AI use policy" for your business: which data never goes into AI, which plan holds client data, and who approves outbound messages.

**Bring to your session:** your Business Profile, your customer profile, the model comparison table and your draft AI use policy.

**Your coach will:**
- Tighten your profile so it removes the generic answers you're still getting
- Review your AI use policy against your client contracts and data
- Recommend the plan (personal vs. Workspace) that fits where your business is now
:::
