# Exercise 3: Build Your Business Brain Notebook

:::key-concept
You'll build a notebook in Gemini Notebook (formerly NotebookLM) from your business files and research, then use it to write a cited FAQ and an audio briefing. At the end you'll have a "Business Brain" notebook and a customer FAQ Doc where every answer traces back to a source.
:::

**Time:** 30 minutes · **You'll need:** A Google account at notebooklm.google, your Business Profile and Competitor Brief Docs, 2 to 5 other business files or web pages · **Builds on:** Lesson 3

## Warm-up

:::checkpoint
Q: Your notebook can't find an answer to "Do we offer weekend support?" What does that tell you?
- [ ] The notebook is broken and you should switch tools
- [ ] You should ask it to guess based on similar businesses
- [x] Your sources don't cover it, so you need to decide the policy and write it down
Why: A notebook answers only from your sources. A missing answer is a gap in your documents, which is useful to know.
:::

## Steps

1. **Create a notebook** at [notebooklm.google](https://notebooklm.google/) called "Business Brain."
2. **Add sources.** Add your Business Profile and Competitor Brief from Drive. Add 2 to 5 more: your website or service page, a price list, a policy, a competitor's FAQ page. Using the Groomer Books sample? Add three competitor pages from your brief.
3. **Remove anything sensitive** before you upload: account numbers, client IDs, passwords.
4. **Ask a source check question:**

```text
Based only on these sources, list what I offer, who it's for, and what it costs.
Cite each item. If something isn't stated, write "not stated."
```

5. **Find the gaps.** Ask:

```text
What would a new customer want to know that none of these sources answer?
List the top 10 missing answers.
```

6. **Write the missing answers.** Create a Google Doc called "Policies and Answers," write a short answer to each gap in your own words, and add it to the notebook as a source.
7. **Generate a customer FAQ:**

```text
Write a customer FAQ with 10 questions and answers, based only on my sources (not competitor pages).
Keep each answer under 60 words. Cite sources.
```

Copy it into a Doc called "Customer FAQ" and check each citation.
8. **Make an Audio Overview** in Studio with this instruction: "Brief a new contractor on what this business does and how we treat customers. Under 8 minutes." Listen to the first two minutes and note anything wrong.

## Done when

- [ ] A "Business Brain" notebook with at least 5 sources exists
- [ ] You've written a "Policies and Answers" Doc covering the gaps it found
- [ ] A "Customer FAQ" Doc has 10 answers, each traced to your own sources
- [ ] You've generated an Audio Overview and noted any errors

:::coached
**For 1-on-1 coaching clients.** Make the notebook useful to someone other than you:

1. Build a second, smaller notebook for a future contractor with only what they need (no pricing strategy, no client data).
2. Generate flashcards or a quiz from it and take the quiz yourself to find unclear policies.
3. Draft a one-page "how we work" SOP from the notebook and mark any steps that rely on your memory rather than a document.

**Bring to your session:** both notebooks (shared view access), your Customer FAQ and the SOP draft.

**Your coach will:**
- Review your FAQ for promises you can't keep or legal wording to check with a lawyer
- Decide with you what a contractor should and shouldn't see
- Pick the next three documents to write so the business runs without your memory
:::
