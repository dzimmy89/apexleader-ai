# Module 1 Quiz: Gemini, NotebookLM and Workspace
Checks that you can pick the right Google AI tool, plan and safety setup for real founder tasks.

---

## Question 1
You're on the free Gemini plan in October 2026 and want Gemini to help with a complex pricing analysis. What should you know first?

- A) Free users get Gemini 4 Argon with lower limits
- B) Free users get only Flash-Lite, so test the task on Pro or Argon with a paid plan before judging Gemini
- C) Free users can't use Gemini at all anymore
- D) The model you pick doesn't change the answer

**Answer:** B

**Explanation:** Since October 9, 2026 the free plan is limited to Flash-Lite; Pro and Gemini 4 Argon come with Google AI Pro and Ultra.

---

## Question 2
Deep Research cites a 2023 review site for a competitor's monthly price. What's the right move?

- A) Use the number as-is because Deep Research verified it
- B) Remove all prices from your analysis
- C) Ask Deep Research to rerun until the number changes
- D) Open the competitor's own pricing page today and record what it says with the date

**Answer:** D

**Explanation:** Prices change and secondhand sources go stale, so confirm numbers at the source before you rely on them.

## Question 3
You need the exact cancellation notice period from your own client agreement. Which tool fits best?

- A) A notebook in Gemini Notebook (formerly NotebookLM) loaded with your agreement, asked to quote and cite the line
- B) A fresh Gemini chat with no files
- C) Deep Research on industry-standard notice periods
- D) Gemini in Sheets with the AI function

**Answer:** A

**Explanation:** A notebook answers only from your sources and cites the passage, so you get your policy rather than a general average.

## Question 4
You have 400 customer messages in a Google Sheet and want to know how many are complaints. What's the most reliable approach?

- A) Paste them into a chat and ask for a total
- B) Ask Gemini in Gmail to search for "complaint"
- C) Use the AI function to label each row, spot-check a sample, then count with a filter or pivot
- D) Guess from the first 20 rows

**Answer:** C

**Explanation:** Row-by-row labels are visible and checkable, and the count comes from the sheet rather than an AI estimate.

## Question 5
You rely on three Gems you built last year. What should you do in light of Google's September 2026 announcement?

- A) Nothing, Gems will keep working forever
- B) Rebuild your most-used Gems as skills now and test them, rather than waiting for automatic migration
- C) Delete them because skills can't do the same job
- D) Move them into Google Sheets

**Answer:** B

**Explanation:** Skills are replacing Gems, with Gems moving to Settings on November 17, 2026 and business accounts losing them no sooner than March 2027.

## Question 6
You set up a scheduled Gemini task to handle weekly lead follow-ups. Which instruction best follows the agent safety rules?

- A) Send follow-ups automatically so nothing slips
- B) Log in with my main Google password whenever you need access
- C) Pay any overdue invoices you find while you're in the inbox
- D) Draft follow-ups into my tracker; don't send, delete, archive or buy anything

**Answer:** D

**Explanation:** Agents draft and you send; anything that leaves the business needs a human click, and agents never move money.

## Question 7
A Workspace-based founder wants an agent that has its own email, calendar and Drive and only sees files shared with it. Which Google offering does that describe?

- A) Persistent agent coworkers in Google's Gemini agent for work, announced October 8, 2026 under Gemini Enterprise
- B) Gemini Notebook Audio Overviews
- C) The Gemini app's Flash-Lite model
- D) Gemini for Windows

**Answer:** A

**Explanation:** Google's Gemini agent lets managers create persistent agent coworkers with their own Workspace accounts, starting in private preview.

## Question 8
You want Gemini's Tasks feature to watch your inbox in the background, but you only have a Workspace work account. What does Google's help page say?

- A) Tasks work on every account type and plan
- B) Tasks require a personal account with Google AI Pro or Ultra; work accounts aren't supported yet
- C) Tasks only work on the free plan
- D) Tasks only work in Gemini Notebook

**Answer:** B

**Explanation:** As of October 2026, Tasks need a personal Google account on AI Pro or Ultra, age 18+, in a supported country.
