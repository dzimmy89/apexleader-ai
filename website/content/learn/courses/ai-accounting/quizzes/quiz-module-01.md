# Module 1 Quiz: AI Accounting
Checks that you can run books, receipts, invoicing, a cash forecast and tax prep with AI helpers that never move money.

---

## Question 1
Your new expense agent asks for your bank login to match receipts faster. What should you do?

- A) Share it and change the password afterward
- B) Share it only if the agent promises not to make payments
- C) Decline, and give it a limited user that reads transactions through your accounting app's bank feed
- D) Share it but turn off two-factor authentication so it doesn't get stuck

**Answer:** C

**Explanation:** Agents never get bank logins; the accounting app's read-only bank feed gives them what they need without any way to move money.

---

## Question 2
Why should your categories map to Schedule C lines from day one?

- A) So your year-end Profit and Loss already matches the shape of the tax form
- B) Because QuickBooks won't let you use other categories
- C) So you don't need an accountant at all
- D) Because the IRS requires every business to use exactly 15 categories

**Answer:** A

**Explanation:** Matching categories to the form turns tax season into running a report, though your accountant still reviews the return.

---

## Question 3
Your Grok Bot Expense Bot finds a failed payment on a vendor portal and asks to update the card on file and retry. What's the right response?

- A) Allow once, since it's a small amount
- B) Deny it, and update the payment method yourself
- C) Click Always allow so it can handle failed payments in the future
- D) Give it a virtual card number in chat so it can finish

**Answer:** B

**Explanation:** Changing payment methods and paying are money movements, so the agent reports them and you do them yourself.

---

## Question 4
You set up a scheduled Claude Cowork task to file receipts every Friday at 6pm, but it never runs. What's the most likely cause?

- A) Cowork can only run tasks on weekdays before noon
- B) The receipts are in PDF format
- C) Scheduled tasks need your computer awake and the Claude desktop app open
- D) Cowork needs access to your whole hard drive to schedule tasks

**Answer:** C

**Explanation:** Anthropic's help center says Cowork scheduled tasks run only while the computer is awake and the desktop app is open.

---

## Question 5
Your receivables agent proposes changing a client's invoice to a new payment link because the old one "looks expired." What should happen?

- A) Let the agent update it, since faster payment helps cash flow
- B) Ask the agent to email the client the new link
- C) Approve it as long as the amount doesn't change
- D) Deny it, check the link yourself in your invoicing app, and fix it by hand if needed

**Answer:** D

**Explanation:** Changed payment details are a classic fraud pattern, so only you edit payment instructions.

---

## Question 6
A client always pays about three weeks after the due date. Their next invoice is due in week 2 of your forecast. Where should that cash go?

- A) Around week 5, based on their payment history
- B) Week 2, because that's the due date
- C) Leave it out until it's paid, even if they always pay
- D) Split it evenly across all 13 weeks

**Answer:** A

**Explanation:** A cash forecast predicts when money actually arrives, so real payment history beats the due date.

## Question 7
Gemini in Sheets inserts a chart of your closing cash. Next Monday you update the numbers. What should you know?

- A) The chart updates itself in real time
- B) Gemini-inserted charts go on a new tab and don't update when the source data changes, so rebuild or create it with Insert, Chart
- C) The chart deletes itself after 24 hours
- D) You must rerun the whole forecast in Gemini every week

**Answer:** B

**Explanation:** Google's help page says charts Gemini inserts don't update when the original data changes.

## Question 8
Your year-end agent writes, "You can deduct 100% of client meals." What's the right way to use that?

- A) Recategorize all meals as Advertising to be safe
- B) File your return with that assumption, since the agent read every receipt
- C) Add it to your questions for your accountant and let them confirm the rule
- D) Ask the agent to file the return so it can apply the rule correctly

**Answer:** C

**Explanation:** Tax content from AI is general information; your accountant advises and prepares, and you sign.
