# Module 1 Quiz: Brand in a Day
Checks that you can define a voice, make a logo and imagery responsibly, and wire a brand kit into your agents.

---

## Question 1
Claude suggests "innovative, professional, customer-focused" as your voice. What's the best response?

- A) Accept it, because those are safe traits
- B) Replace them with three or four specific "this, not that" traits based on your own and your customers' words
- C) Add five more adjectives so the agent has more to work with
- D) Skip the voice guide and fix tone in each draft

**Answer:** B

**Explanation:** Generic traits don't change what an agent writes, while a specific contrast like "plain, not dumbed down" is a rule an agent can follow and you can check.

---

## Question 2
Where is the best raw material for finding your brand voice?

- A) Your competitors' homepages, copied closely
- B) A list of popular marketing buzzwords
- C) Your own best writing plus the exact phrases customers use about their problem
- D) Whatever Claude writes when you ask it to sound friendly

**Answer:** C

**Explanation:** Your voice already exists in how you write and how your customers talk; Claude's job is to find the pattern, not invent one.

---

## Question 3
You paste customer call notes into Claude to find voice patterns. What should you do first?

- A) Remove names, contact details and anything shared in confidence
- B) Nothing, since Claude needs full context
- C) Translate them into formal English
- D) Send them to all your agents at once

**Answer:** A

**Explanation:** Keep the customer's words and drop their identity; private details don't belong in AI tools or brand files.

---

## Question 4
You love a logo ChatGPT generated in a single prompt. Which statement is most accurate?

- A) The tool's terms make it exclusively yours, so no further checks are needed
- B) It's automatically a registered trademark
- C) It's illegal to use AI-generated images in business
- D) A prompt-only image may be hard to protect by copyright, so refine it with human design choices and run a trademark search

**Answer:** D

**Explanation:** The US Copyright Office has said prompts alone generally aren't authorship, and trademark conflicts are a separate check that no AI tool does for you.

---

## Question 5
Why rebuild your chosen logo concept as an SVG instead of using the generated PNG?

- A) SVG files are required by trademark law
- B) A vector stays sharp at any size, from a favicon to a banner, and is easy to recolor
- C) PNG files can't be uploaded to websites
- D) SVG files hide the fact that AI was used

**Answer:** B

**Explanation:** Image models output pixels; a vector logo scales cleanly and gives you consistent black, white and color versions.

---

## Question 6
Your accent color on your background scores 2.9:1 contrast. What's the right rule for it?

- A) Use it for all body text since it's on-brand
- B) Delete it from your palette
- C) Use it for shapes, buttons with dark text or very large headings, but not normal-size text
- D) Only use it on social media

**Answer:** C

**Explanation:** WCAG asks for at least 4.5:1 for normal text and 3:1 for large text, so a failing pair can still work for non-text elements.

---

## Question 7
You want every Claude chat to apply your brand rules automatically, and your coding agent to use your colors on the website. What setup fits?

- A) A Claude skill with a clear "use whenever" description, plus a Brand section in your repo's CLAUDE.md or AGENTS.md pointing to the brand folder
- B) Paste the brand guide into every chat and every coding session
- C) Email the brand guide to yourself
- D) Rename your files with the hex codes

**Answer:** A

**Explanation:** A skill loads when the task matches its description in any chat, and the rules file is read at the start of every coding session.

---

## Question 8
Your brand-trained agent drafts a great LinkedIn post. What happens next?

- A) The agent posts it, since the brand kit guarantees quality
- B) You review and approve it, then publish
- C) It waits until a second agent approves it
- D) It's scheduled automatically for next week

**Answer:** B

**Explanation:** A brand kit makes drafts faster, but anything that leaves the business needs your click.
