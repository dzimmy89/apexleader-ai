# Module 2 Quiz: AI Sales
Checks that you can run research agents, sequencers and autonomous outbound agents with approval, caps and honest measurement.

---

## Question 1
You open Explee for the first time. Which setting do you change before anything else?

- A) Raise the daily budget so the test produces enough data
- B) Turn Autopilot off so drafts wait for your review
- C) Switch on every suggested audience to compare them
- D) Connect your main business inbox

**Answer:** B

**Explanation:** Autopilot can be on by default, which lets the agent draft and send without you. Turning it off is what keeps every send behind your review.

---

## Question 2
An Origami row shows a business email, but you can't find it on the salon's website or listing. What should you do?

- A) Send to it, since Origami verified it
- B) Mark the source "unknown," look for a published contact, and leave the row out of the sequence until you find one
- C) Ask Origami to verify it a second time
- D) Guess a more common format such as info@ plus the domain

**Answer:** B

**Explanation:** Verified means likely to be deliverable, not chosen by the business. The course standard is to trace every contact to something the business published.

---

## Question 3
Explee's drafts are signed "Alex, Growth Team," a name you never set. Why does this have to change before sending?

- A) It's off-brand
- B) CAN-SPAM bans misleading header and "from" information, and you're responsible even when a tool sends
- C) Prospects reply less to first names
- D) It doesn't matter as long as the opt-out link works

**Answer:** B

**Explanation:** Sender information must be accurate. Set your real name, business, postal address and opt-out line before the first send.

---

## Question 4
A vendor page says customers typically get "a first positive reply in 8 days." How should you use that number?

- A) Quote it to coaching clients as what they can expect
- B) Use it as your target and stop the channel if you miss it
- C) Treat it as a vendor claim, and judge the channel against your own last two weeks
- D) Add it to your outreach emails as social proof

**Answer:** C

**Explanation:** Vendor figures come from other businesses and markets. Measure your own numbers, and never repeat results claims you can't back up.

---

## Question 5
Why send cold email from a separate sending domain instead of your main business domain?

- A) It's required by CAN-SPAM
- B) Bounces or spam complaints from cold outreach then don't damage the domain you use for clients and invoices
- C) It lets you hide who is sending
- D) It removes the need for SPF, DKIM and DMARC

**Answer:** B

**Explanation:** A separate domain keeps cold-outreach reputation problems away from your main domain. It still needs authentication, your real identity and a reply path you read.

## Question 6
You want to find out quickly whether pet boarding businesses respond to your offer, without building a new list by hand. Which tool fits best?

- A) Your Sales Bot, drafting five emails a day
- B) Explee, with Autopilot off, one audience and a small daily cap
- C) Origami LinkedIn steps at 40 requests a day
- D) A purchased email list of pet businesses

**Answer:** B

**Explanation:** Explee is built for fast audience tests from your domain. The four safety settings keep the test small and reviewed.

---

## Question 7
You decide to use Origami's automated LinkedIn steps. Which setup follows the course?

- A) Connect three LinkedIn profiles and rotate 30 requests a day across them
- B) Connect your own profile only, start at 10 requests a weekday, keep approval on, and pause on any LinkedIn warning
- C) Let your Grok Bot Sales Bot handle LinkedIn so Origami can focus on email
- D) Send pitch messages with every connection request to save a step

**Answer:** B

**Explanation:** LinkedIn's terms ban automation and accounts get restricted for it. If you accept that risk, keep it small: one real profile, low volume, approval on, and a pause rule.

---

## Question 8
The same 15 salons show up in both your Origami sequence and your Explee audience this week. What do you do?

- A) Let both tools contact them
- B) Keep them in one cold channel only and remove them from the other
- C) Move them to LinkedIn instead
- D) Ignore it, since the tools stop on reply

**Answer:** B

**Explanation:** Two tools pitching the same person in one week reads as spam and invites complaints. Each lead belongs to one cold channel at a time.
