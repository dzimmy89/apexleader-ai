# Module 1 Quiz: AI Sales
Checks that you can build a sourced lead list and run outreach, CRM, calls and proposals with agents that wait for your approval.

---

## Question 1
Your research assistant returns 30 prospects, but 3 of the 5 rows you spot-check have contact emails that don't appear anywhere on the business's own site. What should you do?

- A) Send to all 30, since bounces are normal
- B) Tighten the prompt to list only published contacts, mark the rest "unknown," and rerun
- C) Ask the assistant whether the emails are real and keep the ones it confirms
- D) Buy a verified email list for the same businesses instead

**Answer:** B

**Explanation:** Guessed addresses bounce and damage your sender reputation, so only use contacts traced to something the business published.

## Question 2
You want your Grok Bot Sales Bot to save you time without risking a bad batch of emails. Which setup fits the course's rules?

- A) The Bot sends from your main inbox with Always allow turned on for email
- B) The Bot has your main Google password so it can work faster
- C) The Bot drafts into an Outreach queue sheet, and an Ask first rule blocks any send until you approve it
- D) The Bot sends LinkedIn connection requests while you review emails

**Answer:** C

**Explanation:** The Bot gets its own scoped login and drafts only, and any external send needs your explicit approval, never a standing Always allow.

## Question 3
A prospect replies to your outreach: "Please take me off your list." Under CAN-SPAM, what is the requirement?

- A) Honor the opt-out within 10 business days, and keep them off future emails
- B) Nothing, because this was business-to-business email
- C) Reply to confirm, then send one final offer
- D) Honor it only if they click an unsubscribe link

**Answer:** A

**Explanation:** The FTC's guide says CAN-SPAM covers B2B email and opt-outs must be honored within 10 business days.

## Question 4
Which of these is NOT a basic that decides whether your outreach lands in the inbox?

- A) SPF and DKIM records for your domain
- B) A DMARC policy, even if it starts at p=none
- C) Starting at low daily volume and growing slowly
- D) Adding more images and links to each email

**Answer:** D

**Explanation:** Authentication, steady volume and low bounces and complaints drive deliverability; extra images and links tend to look more like bulk mail.

## Question 5
Your CRM agent reads an email where a prospect says "Not now, maybe next spring." What should it do under a well-written apply-vs-propose rule?

- A) Move the deal to Lost and delete the next step
- B) Email the prospect to ask which month in spring works
- C) Delete the row to keep the pipeline clean
- D) Add a dated note and propose a spring next step date, leaving the stage change for you

**Answer:** D

**Explanation:** The agent applies low-risk updates like notes and only proposes judgment calls such as Lost, and it never emails prospects.

## Question 6
Claude Duet drafts a follow-up after your call that includes a discount you never offered. What's the right move?

- A) Approve it, since discounts help close deals
- B) Edit out the discount, approve the corrected email, and tell Duet to restate only what was agreed
- C) Let it send and correct the offer in your next email
- D) Turn off approvals so Duet learns faster from its mistakes

**Answer:** B

**Explanation:** Duet asks for approval before sending messages so you can catch errors, and correcting its preferences keeps the mistake from repeating.

## Question 7
You raise your package price in your Offer facts sheet. What should happen to a proposal you already sent last week?

- A) Nothing: it was frozen as a dated PDF and tagged Sent, and you decide separately whether to honor the old price
- B) Your OpenAI Dot updates the shared Page so the client sees the new price
- C) Your Dot emails the client the new price automatically
- D) You delete the old proposal and send the new one without explanation

**Answer:** A

**Explanation:** Only drafts update automatically, so the client always signs exactly what they read and nothing reaches them without your click.

## Question 8
Which LinkedIn practice is consistent with this course and LinkedIn's terms?

- A) Using a browser extension to scrape profiles into your lead list
- B) Letting your Sales Bot send connection requests overnight
- C) Reading profiles yourself and adding notes to your sheet by hand
- D) Giving an agent your LinkedIn password so it can message prospects

**Answer:** C

**Explanation:** LinkedIn prohibits bots, scrapers and automation that act on the site for you, so use it manually and keep agents off it.
