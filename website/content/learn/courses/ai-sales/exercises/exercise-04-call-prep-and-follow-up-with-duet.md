# Exercise 4: Run a Full Call Cycle With Duet

:::key-concept
You'll set up a call-prep routine in Claude Duet, get a brief for a real or practice call, then turn your notes into an approved follow-up email and CRM update. You'll end with a reusable brief format and a follow-up you actually sent.
:::

**Time:** 30 minutes · **You'll need:** Claude Duet (or regular Claude with Gmail, Calendar and Drive connected if you're still on the waitlist), your Pipeline from Exercise 3 · **Builds on:** Lesson 4

## Warm-up

:::checkpoint
Q: Before your call you want to record it for better notes. What's the right approach?
- [ ] Record silently, since you're only using it internally
- [x] Ask the other person at the start and record only if they agree
- [ ] Record only if your meeting tool shows a notice
Why: Consent rules vary by state and country, and some require everyone's consent. Asking every time is the simple rule that works everywhere.
:::

:::

1. **Pick a call.** Use a real call on your calendar this week. No call yet? Book a 20-minute practice call with a friend who plays a prospect from your Lead list, using a real business name from the sheet.

2. **Connect only what's needed.** Make sure your Claude account has calendar, email and the Drive folder with your Pipeline connected (or HubSpot if that's your CRM). Disconnect anything financial you don't need for this.

3. **Teach the routine.** Paste the sales assistant routine from Lesson 4 into your Duet thread. Edit the brief format to fit your business. Keep it to six sections or fewer.

4. **Get the brief.** Wait for Duet to send it before the call, or ask: "Prepare the brief for my [time] call with [name] now." Without Duet, paste the same request into Claude.

5. **Check the brief.** Click one source link. Confirm the history matches what you remember. Rewrite the "My goal for this call" line if it isn't one specific next step.

6. **Run the call.** Ask your three questions. Agree on one next step with a date.

7. **Send notes within an hour.** Paste five to eight bullet points into the thread and ask for the follow-up email and CRM updates.

8. **Approve with edits.** Remove any promise you didn't make. Make sure the next step and date are in the email. Approve the send (or send it yourself), and approve the CRM update.

9. **Set the watch.** Ask Duet to remind you if your next step slips and to draft a check-in if there's no reply after three business days.

10. **Save the brief format** as a doc called "Call brief format" so you can reuse it anywhere.

## Done when

- [ ] You have a saved "Call brief format" doc with six or fewer sections
- [ ] A brief was produced and you verified at least one source in it
- [ ] Your follow-up was sent within a few hours of the call, after your edits and approval
- [ ] The Pipeline row shows the new stage, a dated note, next step and next step date
- [ ] A reminder or check-in watch is set for the next step

:::coached
**For 1-on-1 coaching clients.** Turn calls into a repeatable process.

1. Run the full cycle on three real calls this week.
2. After the third, ask for the pattern review prompt from Lesson 4 and write down the most common objection.
3. Draft a one-paragraph answer to that objection and add it to your brief format.

**Bring to your session:** the three briefs, your notes and follow-ups, and your objection answer.

**Your coach will:**
- Role-play the objection with you live
- Review your follow-ups for clarity and next-step strength
- Help you decide which call stage to tighten first
:::
