# Exercise 6: Run Your First Origami Play

:::key-concept
You'll turn your ICP into a saved Origami prompt, run one signal play, check the sources, and move the 20 best rows into your Lead list with the source tool recorded. At the end your Lead list has a second, faster source that feeds everything else in this module.
:::

**Time:** 40 minutes · **You'll need:** Your ICP doc and Lead list sheet from Exercise 1, an Origami account (a paid plan is needed for contact details and export) · **Builds on:** Lesson 6

## Warm-up

:::checkpoint
Q: What is a "play" in Origami?
- [ ] A pre-written email template
- [x] One way of getting customers, such as businesses with a recent buying signal
- [ ] A paid add-on for LinkedIn
Why: Origami defines a play as "one way of getting customers." Each play is a different way of finding the right leads, such as signals, website visitors or a new market.
:::

:::

1. **Write the prompt.** In your ICP doc, add a section called "Origami prompt" with four parts: Find (who and where), Include (size), Signal (one "why now" event with a time window), Exclude (your disqualifiers). Use the Lesson 6 example as a model. Ask for a source link for each fact.

2. **Create a workspace.** In Origami, start a new search or workspace named "Rolling Ledger: hiring signal" (or your own business and signal). Paste the prompt.

3. **Read the first 10 rows.** Before fetching more, count how many of the first 10 are wrong fits. If more than two are, add what went wrong to Exclude and rerun. Note how many reruns you needed.

4. **Pull 30 matches.** Let it return up to 30 businesses. Don't request phone numbers.

5. **Prune, then enrich.** Delete franchises, chains, restaurant groups and anything else your profile rules out. Then request emails for the rows you kept. Write down the credits or plan usage shown before and after.

6. **Spot-check five.** Pick five rows at random. For each, open the source link and confirm (a) the signal is real and within your time window, and (b) the email appears on the business's own site or listing. Record Pass or Fail in a new column.

7. **Import the keepers.** Export to CSV. Copy the matching columns into your Lead list. Add two columns: **Source tool** ("Origami") and **Play** ("hiring signal"). Check every row against your Do not contact tab.

8. **Score.** Apply the Lesson 1 formula (Fit 1 to 3, plus Signal 0 to 2). Keep the top 20 and set their Status to "New."

## Done when

- [ ] Your ICP doc has a saved Origami prompt with Find, Include, Signal and Exclude lines
- [ ] 20 scored rows are in your Lead list, each with a source URL, Source tool and Play
- [ ] Five spot-checks are recorded, with at least four passes (or you reran until you got there)
- [ ] You wrote down what the run cost in credits or plan usage
- [ ] No row has a home address or personal cell number

:::coached
**For 1-on-1 coaching clients.** Compare two plays on your real business.

1. Run a second play of a different type (for example "new market" instead of "buying signal") with the same Include and Exclude lines.
2. For each play, record: rows returned, rows kept after pruning, spot-check pass rate, and credits used.
3. Write two sentences on which play you'd run every month and why.

**Bring to your session:** both prompts, your comparison numbers, and your top 10 leads.

**Your coach will:**
- Tighten your prompt and disqualifiers
- Check your spot-checks and data handling
- Help you pick the play to run monthly
:::
