# Exercise 8: The $10 Explee Test

:::key-concept
You'll set up Explee with the four safety settings from Lesson 8, prune it to one audience, review its first 10 drafts, and let it send one day's capped budget. At the end you'll have a Test log with real numbers and a written decision: keep testing, fix and retest, or stop.
:::

**Time:** 35 minutes, plus a check the next day · **You'll need:** An Explee account (new accounts get free credits; if you add a card, use one you can monitor), your ICP doc, your compliant footer from Exercise 2 · **Builds on:** Lesson 8

## Warm-up

:::checkpoint
Q: Which of these is required in every commercial email under CAN-SPAM, even when a tool like Explee sends it?
- [ ] A link to your pricing page
- [x] Accurate sender information, a valid postal address and a way to opt out
- [ ] A personal detail about the recipient
Why: The FTC guide requires honest header and "from" information, a postal address, and a working opt-out. You stay responsible even when a tool sends for you.
:::

:::warning
Only send real emails for a real business you run. If you're working with the Rolling Ledger sample business, do steps 1 to 5 to see how the tool behaves, then stop. Don't add credit or let it send on behalf of a business that doesn't exist.
:::

1. **Add a Test log tab** to your Lead list workbook with these columns:

```text
Date | Audience | Emails sent | Spend | Replies | Positive replies | Opt-outs | Bounced or stale | Notes
```

2. **Start Explee.** Paste your business domain. Don't add credit yet if the tool lets you explore first.

3. **Change the four settings.** Before anything sends:
   - Autopilot: **off**
   - Daily budget: **$10** (up to $30 if $10 is below the minimum)
   - Sender name and signature: **your real name, business, postal address and opt-out line**
   - Audiences switched on: **one**
   Screenshot each setting. If you can't turn Autopilot off or set your real name, stop here and write that in Notes.

4. **Prune the audiences.** Delete any suggested segment your ICP disqualifies. Keep one. Open 10 sample prospects and count wrong fits. If more than two, edit the segment description and check again.

5. **Review 10 drafts.** For each: check one detail against the prospect's site, confirm the offer is described correctly, confirm the footer, and remove any claim about results. Edit or reject as needed.

6. **Send one day.** Using your free credits (or $10 to $30 you add), switch on the one campaign and let it run for one day at your daily cap.

7. **Log the results the next day.** Fill one row in Test log. Count "Bounced or stale" from bounce notices and replies like "no longer here" or "we closed." Work out cost per positive reply by hand, or write "none yet."

8. **Decide.** Under the log, write one line: Keep testing, Fix and retest, or Stop, plus the reason. Check your card statement for the charge.

## Done when

- [ ] Screenshots show Autopilot off, a $10 to $30 cap, your real sender details and one audience
- [ ] 10 drafts were reviewed, with any edits noted
- [ ] One day of results is logged in Test log, including stale contacts
- [ ] Cost per positive reply is calculated by hand (or marked "none yet")
- [ ] A written Keep, Fix or Stop decision sits under the log
- [ ] Any charge on your card matches what you set

:::coached
**For 1-on-1 coaching clients.** Run a one-week head-to-head.

1. Run the same Explee audience for five weekdays at a fixed daily cap.
2. Over the same week, keep your Origami sequence from Exercise 7 running.
3. Compare the two in a table: spend, positive replies, opt-outs, stale contacts, and your minutes spent reviewing.

**Bring to your session:** both logs and the comparison table.

**Your coach will:**
- Review Explee's drafts and audience with you
- Check sender details and compliance
- Help you decide which tool keeps the channel
:::
