# Exercise 7: Build a Sequence and Approve Your First Five

:::key-concept
You'll turn on approval in Origami, connect a separate sending inbox, build a three-step email sequence for your Exercise 6 leads, and approve the first five messages yourself. If you choose to add LinkedIn steps, you'll set them up inside the limits from Lesson 7. At the end you have a sequence that sends follow-ups on time and never sends anything you haven't read.
:::

**Time:** 40 minutes · **You'll need:** Your Lead list with Origami rows from Exercise 6, your compliant footer from Exercise 2, an Origami paid plan, a sending inbox on a separate domain (or Origami's sending inboxes) · **Builds on:** Lesson 7

## Warm-up

:::checkpoint
Q: A lead replies "not interested" to step 1 of your Origami sequence. What happens to steps 2 and 3?
- [x] They don't send, because any reply removes the lead from the sequence
- [ ] They still send on schedule
- [ ] Origami asks the lead to confirm
Why: Origami stops the sequence for a lead as soon as they reply. You still add them to your Do not contact tab so no other tool emails them.
:::

## Steps

:::warning
Only send real messages for a real business you run. If you're working with the Rolling Ledger sample business, build and review everything through step 7, but leave the sequence paused and don't send.
:::

1. **Turn on approval.** Find Origami's approval setting and turn it on so every message waits for you. Screenshot it and save the image in your sales folder.

2. **Connect the sending inbox.** Connect an inbox on a separate sending domain, or let Origami set up sending inboxes. Confirm SPF, DKIM and DMARC for that domain using the steps from Exercise 2, and send a test email to yourself. Check that replies reach an inbox you read every day.

3. **Set the cap.** Set the daily limit to 10 new emails per inbox with warmup on.

4. **Write the three steps.** Create a sequence named "Rolling Ledger: hiring signal." Add:
   - Step 1, day 0: under 120 words, one specific detail, one line on what you do, one small question
   - Step 2, day 3: under 80 words, one useful tip related to their signal
   - Step 3, day 7: under 50 words, a polite close
   Add your footer (real name, business, postal address, opt-out line) to every step. If you let Origami personalize, paste the voice rules from Lesson 7 first.

5. **Decide on LinkedIn.** Write one sentence in your sales folder: "I am / am not using automated LinkedIn steps, because..." If you are:
   - Connect only your own LinkedIn profile
   - Add a connection request (no note) on day 0 and a two-line message 3 days after acceptance
   - Set the LinkedIn limit to 10 connection requests a weekday
   - Write your pause rule: "Any LinkedIn warning or ID check: pause LinkedIn steps for 2 weeks"

6. **Enroll 20 leads.** Add the 20 leads you scored in Exercise 6.

7. **Approve five.** Review the first five drafted messages. For each, check the detail against the lead's site, confirm the footer, and edit at least one. Approve, edit or reject, and log each in your Outreach queue tab with a note.

8. **Check the next morning.** Confirm only approved messages went out, and log any replies, opt-outs or bounces.

## Done when

- [ ] Approval is on, with a screenshot saved
- [ ] The sending domain passes SPF, DKIM and DMARC, and replies reach you
- [ ] The daily cap is 10 per inbox, with warmup on
- [ ] Three steps exist, each with your real name, postal address and opt-out line
- [ ] You wrote down your LinkedIn decision; if yes, one profile, 10 a weekday and a pause rule are set
- [ ] Five messages are reviewed and logged, with at least one edited

:::coached
**For 1-on-1 coaching clients.** Run the sequence for 10 business days on your real list.

1. Log daily: sent, replied, positive replies, opt-outs, bounces, and (if used) LinkedIn requests sent and accepted.
2. Track your approval time each day in minutes.
3. List the three edits you made most often and fold them into your templates or voice rules.

**Bring to your session:** your log, your approval-time total, and the updated templates.

**Your coach will:**
- Review your copy for specificity and compliance
- Check your caps, sending domain and LinkedIn limits
- Decide with you whether to raise any cap, change the play, or stop LinkedIn steps
:::
