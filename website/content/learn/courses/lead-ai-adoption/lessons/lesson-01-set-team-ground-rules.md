# Lesson 1: Set Team Ground Rules

:::key-concept
Your team is already using AI, with or without rules. A one-page policy (which tools, what data never goes in, what always gets checked, and who to ask) makes that use safe and lets people share what works.
:::

## What you'll be able to do

- Write a one-page AI policy for your team.
- Decide which data never goes into an AI tool.
- Set up a simple way for the team to share prompts that work.

## Why this matters

Without rules, one person pastes a client list into a free tool and another refuses to touch AI at all. Clear rules remove both problems.

## The one-page policy

1. **Approved tools**: the ones you have checked and pay for.
2. **Never put in**: passwords, payment details, health data, anything a client asked you to keep private.
3. **Always check**: anything sent to a customer or that changes money.
4. **Ask**: who on the team answers questions and keeps the policy current.

## Share what works

Keep one shared folder of prompts and examples. Spend ten minutes in a weekly meeting on one new use someone found.

:::takeaways
- Approved tools, banned data, required checks, one owner.
- Keep it to one page and review it each quarter.
- Share wins weekly so good habits spread.
:::
