# Exercise 1: Draft Your AI Policy

:::key-concept
You'll draft your team's one-page AI policy and test it against three real situations.
:::

**Time:** 20 minutes · **You'll need:** a document and an AI assistant · **Builds on:** Lesson 1

## Warm-up

:::checkpoint
Q: A team member wants to paste a client's signed contract into a free AI tool to summarize it. What should your policy say?
- [ ] Fine, it saves time
- [x] Only in an approved tool, and only if the contract allows it
- [ ] Never use AI for contracts
Why: The tool and the client's terms decide whether it is allowed.
:::

## Steps

1. Write the four sections: approved tools, never put in, always check, who to ask.
2. Write down three situations your team has run into.
3. Check that the policy answers each one. Fix it where it does not.

## What to bring to your session

Your draft policy and the three situations.
