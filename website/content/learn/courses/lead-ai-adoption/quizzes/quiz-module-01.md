# Module 1 Quiz: Rolling AI Out
Check that you can set rules that keep your team's AI use safe and useful.

---

## Question 1
Which belongs in the "never put in" section of a team AI policy?

- A) A draft blog post
- B) Customer payment card numbers
- C) A public product description
- D) Meeting notes about the office move

**Answer:** B

**Explanation:** Payment details must never go into an AI tool.

---

## Question 2
What is the main reason to write an AI policy?

- A) To stop the team using AI
- B) To make the use that is already happening safe and shareable
- C) Because software vendors require one
- D) To replace training

**Answer:** B

**Explanation:** People already use AI; clear rules make that use safe and let good habits spread.
