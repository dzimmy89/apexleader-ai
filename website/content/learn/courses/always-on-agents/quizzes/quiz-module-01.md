# Module 1 Quiz: Always-On Agents: Duet, Dots and Grok Bots
Checks whether you can choose, set up and safely supervise always-on agents.

---

## Question 1
A client emails at 9 a.m. and your Claude Duet flags it around noon. What is the most likely reason?

- A) Duet only runs overnight
- B) Duet checks connected accounts every few hours, so items can arrive between checks
- C) Duet needs the app open on your phone to work
- D) The email was blocked by the sensitive-data classifier

**Answer:** B

**Explanation:** Anthropic says Duet checks your accounts every few hours rather than continuously, so urgent items can land between checks.

---

## Question 2
You are a US founder on Claude Pro. Which statement about Claude Duet is accurate as of October 2026?

- A) It is generally available to all Claude plans worldwide
- B) It costs a separate monthly fee
- C) It is a research preview by waitlist, and its background checks use your normal Claude limits
- D) It lets you pick a cheaper model to save usage

**Answer:** C

**Explanation:** Duet is a research preview for US Pro and Max accounts, admitted in waves, and the brief and checks count toward your usage limits.

---

## Question 3
You want your dot to never send client emails without you, but to save research to your pages freely. Which Custom rules fit?

- A) Ask before taking action for client emails, and Take action without asking for saving research
- B) Take action without asking for both
- C) Hand off to you for research, and Take action without asking for emails
- D) No rules, since dots never send email

**Answer:** A

**Explanation:** Sends that leave the business need approval, while low-risk internal work can run without asking.

---

## Question 4
You wrote "refresh every Monday" at the top of a ChatGPT Space page. Nothing refreshes. Why?

- A) Space pages cannot be edited by dots
- B) You need ChatGPT Enterprise for any scheduling
- C) The dot is paused by default on weekends
- D) Writing a cadence on a page schedules nothing; you must set up a scheduled task in chat and check it under Scheduled

**Answer:** D

**Explanation:** OpenAI's docs say Keep Updated was not available at launch and recurring work needs a real scheduled task.

---

## Question 5
Your Research Bot and your Billing Bot both run on Grok Bot. Billing is signed in to your invoicing site. What can the Research Bot reach?

- A) The invoicing site, because all your Bots share one computer, its browser sessions and logins
- B) Nothing outside public websites
- C) Only what you approved with Allow once
- D) Only its own files

**Answer:** A

**Explanation:** Grok Bot's docs say all of a user's Bots share one computer and that separate Bots are not a security boundary.

---

## Question 6
A Grok Bot routine runs at 6 a.m. and asks for approval to send a supplier email. You see it at 8 a.m. What happened?

- A) The email was sent automatically after 10 minutes
- B) The approval expired after about 10 minutes and the action did not run, so you ask the Bot to try again
- C) The Bot deleted the routine
- D) The Bot switched to Always allow

**Answer:** B

**Explanation:** Approvals from unattended work expire after about 10 minutes, show as Expired, and do not run.

---

## Question 7
Your dot misbehaves and you press Pause. What does Pause not stop?

- A) The current main task
- B) Your ability to chat with it
- C) Delegated tasks and future scheduled runs
- D) Its access to your Space pages

**Answer:** C

**Explanation:** Pause stops the main task only; stop delegated tasks in Activity and disable recurring ones in Scheduled.

---

## Question 8
After four clean weeks, which promotion is appropriate for an always-on agent?

- A) Let it send client follow-ups without approval
- B) Connect your payment wallet so it can pay small bills
- C) Remove all Ask first rules
- D) Let it file receipts into a folder without asking

**Answer:** D

**Explanation:** Promote only narrow internal actions; sends to customers and anything with money always stay behind your approval.
