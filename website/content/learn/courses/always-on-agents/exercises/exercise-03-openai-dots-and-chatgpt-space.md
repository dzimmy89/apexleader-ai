# Exercise 3: Write Your Dot's Rulebook and First Space Page

:::key-concept
You'll write a complete Custom rules set for a dot, decide which plugins it gets, and build a "Weekly Pipeline" page in ChatGPT Space that refreshes on a schedule. If you don't have a dot yet, you'll still finish with a ready-to-paste rulebook and a page plan.
:::

**Time:** 30 minutes · **You'll need:** ChatGPT Pro or Business Premium with a dot (full path) or any ChatGPT plan (planning path), your Job Spec from Exercise 1 · **Builds on:** Lesson 3

## Warm-up

:::checkpoint
Q: You want your dot to draft invoices but never send them without you. Which rule behavior fits sending invoices?
- [ ] Take action without asking
- [ ] Take action when you say so, then ask it to draft
- [x] Ask before taking action
Why: Invoices leave the business and involve money, so every send needs your approval. Asking for drafts does not authorize sending.
:::

## Steps

1. **Check eligibility.** Dots are for ChatGPT Pro (adults 18+, not in the EEA, Switzerland or the UK) and Business Premium; Enterprise is a beta your admin must enable. If you are eligible, create your dot in the ChatGPT app or desktop web and name it after its role.

2. **Pick plugins.** From your Job Spec, list the apps the dot needs. Connect only those (on mobile: profile, **Customize > Plugins**). Review each plugin's permissions before connecting. No banking, payroll or payment plugins.

3. **Write your rulebook.** Fill in one line per rule, using the four behaviors:

```text
TAKE ACTION WITHOUT ASKING:
- Save research and summaries to my Space pages
TAKE ACTION WHEN I SAY SO:
- Create calendar holds for my own internal work
ASK BEFORE TAKING ACTION:
- Sending any email, Slack or Teams message outside my business
- Creating, editing or sending invoices or quotes
- Sharing any page or file with anyone
- Deleting anything
HAND OFF TO ME:
- Payments, refunds, bank or payroll
- Signing in, accepting terms, signing documents
```

4. **Enter the rules** (desktop and web: **Settings > Personalization > Custom rules**; mobile: **Customize > Custom rules**). Select **Add**, describe the action, choose the behavior, save. Read the default rules already listed.

5. **Build the Space page.** In ChatGPT Space, create a page and mention your dot:

```text
@[your dot] Create a page called "Weekly Pipeline". From my connected email
and calendar, list this week's new leads and booked calls in a table:
name, source, last contact, next step. Do not contact anyone.
```

6. **Schedule the refresh.** In chat, ask: "Set up a scheduled task to refresh my Weekly Pipeline page every Monday at 8 a.m." Then open **Scheduled** in the dot's profile, check the timing and destination, and confirm after the first real run.

7. **Find your off switches.** Locate **Pause** in the profile's ••• menu, the **Activity** list, and **Scheduled**. Write one line each on what they stop.

8. **Planning path.** No dot yet? Save the rulebook and the page prompt. In a regular ChatGPT project, paste the rulebook into the project instructions and run the page prompt manually.

## Done when

- [ ] Every rule uses one of the four behaviors, and sends, shares and deletes are "Ask before"
- [ ] Money, logins and signatures are "Hand off to me"
- [ ] Only the plugins your Job Spec needs are connected, with no payment tools
- [ ] The Weekly Pipeline page exists (or is fully planned) with a verified schedule
- [ ] You know what Pause does not stop, and where to stop scheduled runs

:::coached
**For 1-on-1 coaching clients.** Put the dot to work with your real pipeline.

1. Run the Weekly Pipeline page for two Mondays and compare it to your own records.
2. Add one project the dot moves forward between your conversations (for example, updating a proposal template as your offer changes), with a gate before anything is shared.
3. If you work with a contractor, decide what they may see in Space and test sharing with view-only access.

**Bring to your session:** the rulebook, two weeks of the page, and notes on any surprises in Activity.

**Your coach will:**
- Check your rules for gaps and conflicts
- Review the page for accuracy against your records
- Help you decide whether a dot or another agent owns this job long term
:::
