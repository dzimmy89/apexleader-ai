# Exercise 2: Run Five Interviews and Score Your Assumptions

:::key-concept
You'll write a Mom Test style script, book and run at least five customer interviews, and use AI to synthesize the transcripts. At the end you'll have Part 2 of your Validated Offer Doc: evidence for or against each of your top three assumptions, with real quotes.
:::

**Time:** 45 minutes · **You'll need:** Your Validated Offer Doc, an AI chat, a way to record and transcribe calls (Google Meet note-taking on an eligible plan, a phone recorder, or a transcription app), a notebook tool such as Gemini Notebook · **Builds on:** Lesson 2

The 45 minutes covers prep and synthesis. Book the interviews themselves over the next week.

## Warm-up

:::checkpoint
Q: Which question passes The Mom Test?
- [ ] "Would you pay $150 a month for monthly bookkeeping?"
- [ ] "Do you think a bookkeeping service for groomers is a good idea?"
- [x] "Tell me about the last time you did your books. How long did it take?"
Why: It asks about a specific past event, not an opinion about your idea or a promise about the future.
:::

## Steps

1. **Write the script.** In an AI chat, paste your top three assumptions from Part 1 and use the script prompt from Lesson 2. Then ask: "Which of these questions would a strict Mom Test reviewer reject? Fix them." Save the final script in Part 2.
2. **Build a list of 15 people** who have the problem now: warm intros first, then communities (read the rules), then local walk-ins or direct messages. Track them in a simple sheet: name, where found, contacted, booked.
3. **Draft outreach with AI, send it yourself:**

```text
Write a 70-word message asking [type of person] for a 20-minute call about how they handle [problem].
Make clear I'm researching, not selling. Offer [small thank-you]. Plain and friendly.
Also write a 40-word version for a community post that follows typical "no self-promotion" rules.
```

4. **Run at least five interviews.** Ask permission to record at the start. Stick to the script, follow the energy, and end by asking for an intro to someone else.
5. **Write three surprises** within five minutes of each call.
6. **Prepare transcripts.** Remove last names, phone numbers and anything sensitive. Add all transcripts and your surprise notes to one notebook.
7. **Synthesize:**

```text
Using only these interview transcripts:
1. A table: interviewee (first name only), how they handle [problem] today, time spent, money spent, tools, biggest pain in their words.
2. Top 3 pains with how many people mentioned each and one quote each.
3. For each assumption below, evidence for, evidence against, and a verdict (supported / mixed / not supported):
[paste top 3 assumptions]
4. Who has already paid for a solution, and how much?
```

8. **Check the quotes** against the transcripts, then paste the table, verdicts and best quotes into Part 2. Add a line: "What I'd change about the idea based on this."

## Done when

- [ ] Part 2 holds your final Mom Test script
- [ ] At least five interviews are done, recorded with consent, and transcribed
- [ ] Part 2 has a synthesis table and a verdict on each top-three assumption with quotes
- [ ] You've noted who already pays for a solution and how much
- [ ] You've written what you'd change about the idea

:::coached
**For 1-on-1 coaching clients.** Raise the bar on evidence:

1. Run eight to ten interviews and include at least two people who chose a competitor or "do it yourself."
2. Score each interview on commitment: none, intro given, follow-up booked, wants to buy.
3. Rewrite your top three assumptions based on what you learned and mark which still need evidence.

**Bring to your session:** Part 2, two raw transcripts (anonymized), and your commitment scores.

**Your coach will:**
- Listen for leading questions and missed follow-ups in your transcripts
- Check whether your verdicts match the evidence or your hopes
- Help you decide: go to offer, adjust the idea, or change customer
:::
