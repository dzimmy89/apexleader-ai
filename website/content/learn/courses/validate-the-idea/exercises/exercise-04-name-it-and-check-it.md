# Exercise 4: Name It, Check It and Finish Your Validated Offer Doc

:::key-concept
You'll generate and filter names with AI, test the finalists with customers, and run the four checks: domain, social handles, business-name registry and trademark. At the end your Validated Offer Doc is complete, with a cleared name and a one-page summary you can build on.
:::

**Time:** 30 minutes · **You'll need:** Your Validated Offer Doc (Parts 1 to 3), an AI chat, a domain registrar website, USPTO Trademark Search (tmsearch.uspto.gov) or your country's trademark office · **Builds on:** Lesson 4

## Warm-up

:::checkpoint
Q: Your chosen name is free as a .com. What else must you check before using it?
- [ ] Nothing, a free domain means the name is yours
- [x] Social handles, your state or national business registry, and trademark records for similar names in related fields
- [ ] Only whether an AI assistant says it's trademarked
Why: A domain is only one of four checks. Trademark rights and registered business names are separate, and AI can't reliably see current registries.
:::

## Steps

1. **Generate names.** Use the naming brief from Lesson 4 with your one-sentence offer and two interview quotes from Part 2. Ask for 30 names in five styles.
2. **Cut to eight**, then score each 1 to 5 on Say it, Clear, Distinct, Room to grow and Feels right. Put the scorecard in Part 4.
3. **Test the top three** with at least two interviewees: "Which of these would you trust for [outcome], and why?" Note their answers.
4. **Widen the trademark search with AI:**

```text
For the business name "[name]" offering [services], list 15 spelling and sound-alike variants to search in a trademark database and on Google.
```

5. **Run the four checks** on your top choice and record results in Part 4:

| Check | Where | Result | Date |
|---|---|---|---|
| Domain | Any registrar; .com first, then one variant | | |
| Social handles | The two platforms your customers use most | | |
| Business-name registry | Your state's business entity search (or national register) | | |
| Trademark | tmsearch.uspto.gov: exact name, variants, distinctive word; live marks in related services | | |

6. **Decide.** If any check shows a similar name in a related field, move to your second choice and repeat. If it's close or you plan to invest in branding, note "attorney review needed" in Part 4.
7. **Secure it.** Once a name passes all checks, buy the domain and claim the handles. Use your own account and a strong unique password with two-step verification.
8. **Write the one-page summary** at the top of the doc:

```text
Summarize my Validated Offer Doc in one page:
Name, offer in one sentence, customer, top 3 pains with one quote each, price and tiers, commitment results vs. my pass bar, biggest remaining risk, next 3 actions.
Use only what's in the doc.
```

Edit it and add a final line: "Decision: build / adjust / stop."

## Done when

- [ ] Part 4 has a name scorecard and customer feedback on the top three
- [ ] All four checks are recorded with dates for your chosen name
- [ ] The domain and main handles are secured (or a clear reason why not yet)
- [ ] The Validated Offer Doc has a one-page summary and a build/adjust/stop decision

:::coached
**For 1-on-1 coaching clients.** Get launch-ready:

1. Search your top name in the WIPO Global Brand Database if you plan to sell outside the US, and in app stores and search results for your category.
2. List questions for a trademark attorney: classes to file in, risk of the closest existing marks, whether to file now or after launch.
3. Turn the one-page summary into the brief for your next course (brand, site or product).

**Bring to your session:** your complete Validated Offer Doc, the check results, and your attorney questions.

**Your coach will:**
- Review the full doc end to end and challenge your build/adjust/stop decision
- Flag naming risks to raise with a trademark attorney (general guidance, not legal advice)
- Set your first 30-day plan from the summary
:::
