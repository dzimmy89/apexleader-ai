# Module 1 Quiz: Find and Validate the Idea
Checks that you can research a market, run honest interviews, price an offer and clear a name before you build.

---

## Question 1
You want an honest picture of your idea from a Deep Research run. Which brief is best?

- A) "Is there a big market for my idea?"
- B) "Write a business plan that proves my idea works."
- C) "Who already solves this, what do they charge, what do customers complain about, and what would make my idea fail?"
- D) "Give me the global market size for my industry."

**Answer:** C

**Explanation:** Asking for competitors, complaints and failure reasons pushes the research to test the idea instead of flattering it.

---

## Question 2
Your research report says the global pet care market is worth tens of billions. How should you size your opportunity?

- A) Build a bottom-up estimate from reachable customers, the share you can win in year one and a realistic price
- B) Claim 1 percent of the global market as your target
- C) Use the global number in your pitch as proof of demand
- D) Skip sizing because the market is clearly big

**Answer:** A

**Explanation:** Bottom-up numbers tell a solo founder how many customers they can actually reach and what revenue that means.

---

## Question 3
Which interview question best follows The Mom Test?

- A) "Would you pay $150 a month for this?"
- B) "Walk me through the last time you did your books. How long did it take?"
- C) "Do you think this is a good idea?"
- D) "How much would you pay for something that saved you time?"

**Answer:** B

**Explanation:** The Mom Test asks about specific past behavior rather than opinions about your idea or promises about the future.

---

## Question 4
After a great call, an interviewee says "I'd definitely use that!" but declines to introduce you to anyone or book a follow-up. How should you score it?

- A) Strong demand signal
- B) A signed customer
- C) Reason to start building now
- D) A compliment, not evidence; no commitment was given

**Answer:** D

**Explanation:** Compliments are unreliable; commitments like intros, follow-ups or payments are the real signal.

---

## Question 5
You want to record a customer interview over video and use AI to analyze the transcript. What's the right first step?

- A) Ask the person's permission to record at the start of the call
- B) Record quietly so they act natural
- C) Paste the full transcript with names and phone numbers into any AI tool
- D) Let an AI agent message interviewees for you without review

**Answer:** A

**Explanation:** Get consent before recording, since some places require everyone's consent, and strip personal details before using AI on transcripts.

## Question 6
Which set of anchors gives you the most defensible launch price?

- A) Whatever the cheapest competitor charges, minus 20 percent
- B) The price an AI chat suggests on its first try
- C) The customer's alternatives, the value of the outcome, and your own cost floor
- D) A round number that feels comfortable to say

**Answer:** C

**Explanation:** Alternatives, value and cost floor together keep the price grounded in the market, the customer's outcome and your margins.

---

## Question 7
Ten interviewees loved your offer, but none paid a small refundable deposit. What does this most likely mean?

- A) The offer is validated; start building
- B) The offer, price or audience isn't right yet, so investigate before you build
- C) You should make the offer free
- D) The deposit amount was irrelevant

**Answer:** B

**Explanation:** Real commitments are the test of an offer, and praise without payment usually means the problem or fit isn't strong enough yet.

---

## Question 8
Your favorite name is free as a .com and in your state registry, but a live federal trademark covers a nearly identical name for related services. What should you do?

- A) Use it, because the domain and state registration make it yours
- B) Ask an AI assistant if it's safe and follow its answer
- C) Choose another name or get a trademark attorney's opinion before using it
- D) Change one letter and proceed

**Answer:** C

**Explanation:** A free domain and state registration don't give trademark rights, and a similar mark for related services raises a likelihood of confusion.
