# Exercise 7: A Reference Folder and One Brief

:::key-concept
You'll collect six references, at least three from outside the web, mark one to three qualities on each, and use three of them to brief your agent on one screen. You'll also file one annotated bug report on your own site.
:::

**Time:** 45 minutes · **You'll need:** CleanShot X on a Mac (or Snipping Tool or ShareX on Windows), your phone's camera, your agent with the design system skill, Paper from Exercise 6 · **Builds on:** Lesson 7

## Warm-up

:::checkpoint
Q: What should you ask the agent to take from a reference?
- [ ] The layout
- [ ] The colours and images
- [x] Only the qualities you marked, using your own brand
Why: References are for a quality, like spacing or how numbers line up. Layout, words and images belong to whoever made them, and your brand comes from your design system skill.
:::

## Steps

1. **Make the folder.** Create `design/references/` in your repo.

2. **Collect six references.** At least three from your business's world: a menu, a receipt, a sign, a form, packaging. Photograph or capture each.

3. **Mark each one.** Open each in CleanShot (or your tool), add one to three arrows with short notes, and blur anything private.

4. **Name them by purpose**, like `numbers-right-aligned.png`. List them with their marks in `design/references/README.md`.

5. **Pick one screen** and three references that fit it.

6. **Brief the agent** with the Lesson 7 prompt: the three references, what to take from each, "design-system skill wins," "don't copy layout, words or images," and "draw it in Paper first."

7. **Compare.** Put the Paper frame next to the references. Did it take the qualities you marked? Is it still clearly your brand? Note it in the README.

8. **File one bug.** Capture a real problem on your own site, mark it with one arrow and what you expected, blur anything private, and hand it to the agent with "Fix this and show me a screenshot at 390 wide." Open a pull request for the fix.

## Done when

- [ ] Six references are in `design/references/`, at least three from outside the web, each with one to three marks
- [ ] The README lists each one and what it's for
- [ ] One screen has been drawn in Paper from three references, and you've noted how well it worked
- [ ] One annotated bug report became a pull request

:::example
title: See an example result

For Rolling Ledger, the references were a chalkboard menu, a fuel receipt, a ticket stub, a banking app's month view, a calm pricing page and the founder's own site. The monthly summary brief used the receipt, the month view and the ticket stub. The Paper frame had one big coral total, receipts listed with amounts on the right, and small ink labels over each category total. No receipt textures, no copied layout. The bug report, a Start button below the fold on a phone, was fixed in one pull request.

:::coached
**For 1-on-1 coaching clients.** Build a lasting reference library.

1. Collect ten references from your customers' world.
2. Group them by quality: spacing, type, numbers, tone.
3. Add the best three qualities to your design system skill as rules.

**Bring to your session:** `design/references/` and the Paper frame.

**Your coach will:**
- Check your references are inspiration, not copies
- Help you turn marked qualities into design rules
- Review the frame against your brand
:::
