# Exercise 2: Map Your Workflow

:::key-concept
You'll map your chosen workflow step by step, label each step, and mark where the human check goes.
:::

**Time:** 25 minutes · **You'll need:** paper or a whiteboard · **Builds on:** Lesson 2

## Warm-up

:::checkpoint
Q: Where should the human review go in an automated invoice reminder?
- [ ] At the very start, before the data is pulled
- [x] Just before the reminder is sent to the customer
- [ ] Nowhere, once it is set up
Why: The review belongs right before anything leaves the business.
:::

## Steps

1. Write the trigger that starts the workflow.
2. List every step with its input, action and output.
3. Label each step AI, automation or person.
4. Mark the human check.
5. Circle the first two steps you would build.

## What to bring to your session

The map. We will choose the tools for the circled steps together.
