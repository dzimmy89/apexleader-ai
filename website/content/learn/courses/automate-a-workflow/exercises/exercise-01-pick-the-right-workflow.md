# Exercise 1: Score Three Workflows

:::key-concept
You'll score three workflows from your business on the four questions and estimate the hours each could save.
:::

**Time:** 20 minutes · **You'll need:** a spreadsheet or paper · **Builds on:** Lesson 1

## Warm-up

:::checkpoint
Q: Which workflow is the best first candidate?
- [x] Sending a weekly status email built from a project tracker
- [ ] Negotiating annual contracts with your largest supplier
- [ ] Your once-a-year inventory count
Why: The status email is frequent, rule-based, low-risk and easy to time.
:::

## Steps

1. List three workflows you or your team repeat.
2. Score each 1 to 3 on frequency, rules, risk and measurability.
3. Estimate hours saved a month for each.
4. Pick the winner and write one sentence on why.

## What to bring to your session

Your scores and the workflow you picked.
