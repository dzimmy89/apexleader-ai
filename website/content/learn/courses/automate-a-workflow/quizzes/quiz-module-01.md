# Module 1 Quiz: From Task to Automation
Check that you can choose a good first workflow and map it before you build.

---

## Question 1
A task takes 15 minutes, runs 20 times a month, and you will still spend 3 minutes checking each run. About how many hours a month does automating it save?

- A) 5 hours
- B) 4 hours
- C) 1 hour
- D) 20 hours

**Answer:** B

**Explanation:** (15 − 3) × 20 = 240 minutes, which is 4 hours. The checking time comes off the saving.

---

## Question 2
Which step in a workflow should stay with a person?

- A) Copying a new form entry into a spreadsheet
- B) Summarizing a customer's email
- C) Approving a refund over your usual limit
- D) Formatting a weekly report

**Answer:** C

**Explanation:** Decisions about money and exceptions need human judgment. The others are rule-based or text tasks.

---

## Question 3
Why map a workflow before choosing a tool?

- A) Tools only work with drawn diagrams
- B) The map shows which steps suit AI, which suit simple automation, and where the check belongs
- C) It makes the automation run faster
- D) It is only needed for large teams

**Answer:** B

**Explanation:** Most failed automations come from an unclear process, not a bad tool.
