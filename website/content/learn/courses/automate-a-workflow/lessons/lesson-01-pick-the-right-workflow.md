# Lesson 1: Pick the Right Workflow

:::key-concept
The best first workflow to automate is frequent, rule-based, low-risk and easy to measure. Automating a rare or high-stakes process first wastes time and burns trust.
:::

## What you'll be able to do

- Score a workflow on frequency, rules, risk and measurability.
- Estimate the hours a month an automation could save.
- Choose one workflow to build first.

## Why this matters

An automation that saves two hours a week pays for itself quickly and wins your team over. One that breaks on a big client's order does the opposite.

## The four questions

1. **Frequency**: does it happen every day or week?
2. **Rules**: could you write the steps on one page?
3. **Risk**: if it goes wrong once, is the damage small and easy to fix?
4. **Measurability**: can you time it today and again after?

Score each from 1 to 3. Start with the highest total.

## Estimate the saving

Minutes per run × runs per month ÷ 60 = hours a month. Be honest about the minutes you will still spend checking.

:::takeaways
- Frequent, rule-based, low-risk, measurable.
- Estimate the saving after the time you spend checking.
- Build one workflow well before starting a second.
:::
