# Lesson 2: Map It Before You Build

:::key-concept
Write the workflow down step by step before you touch a tool: what starts it, what each step takes in and puts out, who decides, and where a person checks. The map tells you which steps AI should do, which a simple automation should do, and which stay with a person.
:::

## What you'll be able to do

- Map a workflow as trigger, steps, decisions and outputs.
- Label each step as AI, automation or person.
- Place the human check where a mistake would be expensive.

## Why this matters

Most failed automations skip this step. The tool works; the process it automated was never clear.

## The map

For each step write: input, action, output, and who does it today. Then label it:

- **AI**: reading, writing or summarizing text.
- **Automation**: moving data between tools on a fixed rule, such as a new form entry becoming a row in a spreadsheet.
- **Person**: judgment, money, anything a customer would want a human for.

## Put the check in the right place

Add a human review just before anything leaves your business or changes money. Everywhere else, spot-check a sample each week.

:::try-it
Map your chosen workflow on paper first. If it takes more than one page, split it and automate the first half.
:::

:::takeaways
- Map trigger, steps, decisions and outputs first.
- Label each step AI, automation or person.
- Review before anything leaves the business.
:::
