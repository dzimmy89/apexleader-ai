# Module 1 Quiz: Get Found
Checks that you can set up the free search stack, find the right questions, fix technical basics through an agent, and build honest trust signals.

---

## Question 1
A page you care about gets no search traffic. Which step do you check first?

- A) Whether it's crawled and indexed, in Search Console's URL Inspection
- B) Whether it has enough keywords
- C) Whether it has an llms.txt file
- D) Whether it has FAQ markup

**Answer:** A

**Explanation:** Fix the earliest broken step first. A page that isn't indexed can't rank or be cited in AI answers.

---

## Question 2
What does Search Console's Generative AI performance report show?

- A) Your ranking in ChatGPT
- B) Every question people asked Gemini
- C) Impressions for your pages in AI Overviews, AI Mode and Discover's AI features
- D) Copilot citations

**Answer:** C

**Explanation:** Google launched it in June 2026 to show impressions in its generative AI features, by page, country, device and date.

---

## Question 3
Why does Bing Webmaster Tools matter even if most of your traffic comes from Google?

- A) It's required for Google indexing
- B) It replaces Search Console
- C) It blocks AI crawlers
- D) Copilot grounds its answers in Bing, and its AI Performance report shows Copilot citations

**Answer:** D

**Explanation:** Microsoft's AI answers draw on Bing, and the free AI Performance report shows how often they cite you and the grounding queries behind it.

---

## Question 4
Three buyer questions say the same thing in different words. How many pages should they get?

- A) One page per question
- B) One page that answers all three
- C) None, they're too similar
- D) A page per question in each city you serve

**Answer:** B

**Explanation:** Google's systems understand synonyms. Making a page per small variation adds nothing and can count as scaled content abuse.

---

## Question 5
Your agent's audit finds canonical links pointing at your hosting preview address. Why fix it first?

- A) It slows the page down
- B) It breaks your structured data
- C) It can make search engines treat the preview, not your domain, as the real page
- D) It doesn't matter

**Answer:** C

**Explanation:** The canonical tells search engines which address is the real one. A wrong one can send your indexing and signals to the wrong address.

---

## Question 6
Which Core Web Vitals scores count as good?

- A) LCP under 2.5 s, INP under 200 ms, CLS under 0.1
- B) LCP under 10 s, INP under 2 s, CLS under 1
- C) Any score above 50 in PageSpeed
- D) Only the desktop score matters

**Answer:** A

**Explanation:** Those are Google's published thresholds for a good page experience.

---

## Question 7
An agent offers to add FAQ markup with questions that aren't on the page. What do you do?

- A) Accept, since more markup helps AI
- B) Decline: structured data must match visible content, and FAQ rich results are limited to a few authoritative sites
- C) Accept, but only on the home page
- D) Ask it to add HowTo markup too

**Answer:** B

**Explanation:** Markup for content that isn't visible breaks Google's guidelines, and Google says structured data isn't required for its AI features.

---

## Question 8
Which review request follows Google's policy and the FTC's rules?

- A) "Love us? Leave 5 stars. Unhappy? Email us instead."
- B) "Review us and get 10% off."
- C) "Ask your family to review us."
- D) "Thanks for working with us. We'd value an honest review: [link]."

**Answer:** D

**Explanation:** Google bars incentives and review gating, and the FTC bans fake, bought and undisclosed reviews. Ask everyone, for an honest review.
