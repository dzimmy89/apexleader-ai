# Module 3 Quiz: Get Found
Checks that you understand how AI answers pick sources, how to build citable pages and earn real mentions, and how to measure AI visibility.

---

## Question 1
Which crawler decides whether ChatGPT search can cite your pages?

- A) GPTBot
- B) Google-Extended
- C) ClaudeBot
- D) OAI-SearchBot

**Answer:** D

**Explanation:** OAI-SearchBot controls inclusion in ChatGPT search. GPTBot is OpenAI's training crawler, a separate setting.

---

## Question 2
What does Google say about llms.txt?

- A) Google Search ignores it, and you don't need new machine-readable files
- B) It's required for AI Overviews
- C) It replaces robots.txt
- D) It boosts rankings

**Answer:** A

**Explanation:** Google's guide says Google Search ignores llms.txt. It's harmless for other services but optional.

---

## Question 3
Robots.txt allows PerplexityBot, but your logs show it getting 403 responses. What's wrong?

- A) Nothing
- B) Perplexity ignores small sites
- C) A firewall or CDN setting is blocking it
- D) Your sitemap is too big

**Answer:** C

**Explanation:** A 403 means the server refused the request. Firewalls and CDNs can block bots that robots.txt allows.

---

## Question 4
Which passage is an assistant most likely to cite?

- A) "There are many great options."
- B) "For food truck owners, App A logs fuel and mileage automatically and costs $15 a month (checked November 2026)."
- C) "Click here to learn more."
- D) "We're the best in the business."

**Answer:** B

**Explanation:** It answers directly with specifics an assistant can stand behind: who it's for, the feature, the price and the date.

---

## Question 5
You can't confirm a competitor's price for your comparison table. What goes in the cell?

- A) "Not stated", with the date checked
- B) A guess marked "about"
- C) The price from an old review
- D) Leave the competitor out

**Answer:** A

**Explanation:** Every cell must be verifiable. A wrong fact about a competitor costs more trust than a gap.

---

## Question 6
A forum thread asks which app food truck owners use, and you run one. What's the right move?

- A) Post praise from a second account
- B) Pay a regular poster to mention you
- C) Have an agent post for you
- D) Answer fully as yourself, say you run one of the options, and follow the forum's rules

**Answer:** D

**Explanation:** Honest, disclosed participation is allowed and lasts. Fake accounts get banned and work against you in search.

---

## Question 7
A tracker claims to show exactly how Google's AI ranks you. What should you think?

- A) Trust it because it's paid
- B) Be sceptical: Google says no third-party tool can see its ranking or AI systems; trackers sample answers
- C) Only trust it if it's the most expensive
- D) Use it instead of Search Console

**Answer:** B

**Explanation:** Trackers run prompts and record answers at scale. Useful, but a sample, not a view inside Google.

---

## Question 8
When should a solo founder act on a change in the prompt panel?

- A) After any single answer changes
- B) Only when a tracker confirms it
- C) When the change holds for two monthly runs and shows up in another source
- D) Never

**Answer:** C

**Explanation:** Assistant answers vary from run to run. Act on changes that repeat and appear in more than one source.
