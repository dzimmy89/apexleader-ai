# Module 2 Quiz: Get Found
Checks that you can brief, write and refresh pages with AI without scaled content, and run a weekly SEO agent safely.

---

## Question 1
What is the "gap" in a content brief?

- A) The target word count
- B) The keywords competitors use
- C) What the top results miss and your experience can add
- D) The time until the page ranks

**Answer:** C

**Explanation:** The gap is the reason the page should exist. Google asks for non-commodity content, and assistants cite pages that add something.

---

## Question 2
What does an MCP connection let Claude do for SEO?

- A) Rank your pages directly
- B) Bypass Google's spam policies
- C) Publish pages automatically
- D) Call an SEO data tool, such as a results-page or Search Console service, from inside the chat

**Answer:** D

**Explanation:** MCP connects tools to Claude. It saves copying data, but the free route of copying results works too.

---

## Question 3
Which of these breaks Google's scaled content abuse policy?

- A) Fifty near-identical city pages generated to rank for each city name
- B) One AI-drafted page you edited, answering a real question
- C) Using AI to check grammar
- D) Publishing one page a week

**Answer:** A

**Explanation:** The policy targets mass-producing pages mainly to manipulate rankings, however they're made.

---

## Question 4
Claude's draft includes a statistic that isn't in your brief. What do you do?

- A) Keep it if it sounds right
- B) Remove it, or replace it with a number you can prove or a source you link
- C) Round it to a vaguer figure
- D) Move it to the conclusion

**Answer:** B

**Explanation:** Every fact on the page must come from you or a linked source. Invented statistics damage trust.

---

## Question 5
What is an experience insert?

- A) A stock photo of you
- B) A link to your about page
- C) A testimonial you wrote yourself
- D) Two to four sentences of first-hand experience only you could write, ideally with a number

**Answer:** D

**Explanation:** Experience inserts show first-hand experience, which Google's guidance rewards and assistants quote.

---

## Question 6
A page's impressions and position are steady but clicks fell by a third. What's the likely cause?

- A) Google de-indexed it
- B) Something on the results page, such as an AI Overview, now answers the question
- C) It needs a newer date
- D) Its sitemap broke

**Answer:** B

**Explanation:** You're still shown, but fewer people click. Sharpen the title and opening answer and check the Generative AI report.

---

## Question 7
When should you change a page's "last updated" date?

- A) Every month, to look fresh
- B) Never
- C) Only when you've changed the content meaningfully
- D) Whenever you publish a new page

**Answer:** C

**Explanation:** Updating the date without changing the content misleads readers and doesn't help.

---

## Question 8
What access should your weekly SEO agent have in Search Console?

- A) Its own login as a Restricted user
- B) Owner
- C) Your own login
- D) Full access so it can fix problems

**Answer:** A

**Explanation:** The agent only needs to read. Restricted users can view data but can't change settings or add users.
