# Exercise 2: Build a Question Bank and a Topic Map

:::key-concept
You'll collect at least 40 real buyer questions with sources, mark rough demand, and group them into a topic map. At the end you'll have the list of pages your site needs, in order, which Module 2 turns into content.
:::

**Time:** 45 minutes · **You'll need:** your inbox, Search Console from Exercise 1, Grok or Claude, a Google Ads account for Keyword Planner (free, optional) · **Builds on:** Lesson 2

## Warm-up

:::checkpoint
Q: Grok returns a question with no link. What do you do with it?
- [ ] Keep it, since Grok found it
- [x] Delete it, or find the source yourself before keeping it
- [ ] Rewrite it in better words
Why: The bank holds real questions only. A question with no source may be invented, and pages built on invented questions don't match what buyers ask.
:::

## Steps

1. **Create the sheet.** In your marketing sheet, add a `Question Bank` tab with columns: Question | Source link | Date | Stage (problem, comparison, purchase) | Demand (High, Some, Unknown) | Topic | Target page.

2. **Inbox and calls (10 minutes).** Copy every question prospects and customers asked you in the last three months. Use "email" or "call notes" as the source.

3. **Search Console (5 minutes).** In Performance → Queries, add a filter for queries containing `how`, `what`, `can` or `does`. Copy the questions. New site with no data? Skip this step.

4. **People Also Ask (5 minutes).** Search Google for your three main topics and open the question boxes. Copy the questions and the search you used as the source.

5. **Reddit, X and forums (10 minutes).** Paste the search prompt from Lesson 2 into Grok or Claude Research. Open three of the links yourself to check them. Delete any row whose link doesn't show the question.

6. **Competitor reviews (5 minutes).** Read ten 2- and 3-star reviews of tools or services your buyers use today. Turn each complaint into the question behind it, such as "Is there bookkeeping software that handles tips?"

7. **Mark demand.** Use Search Console impressions, Keyword Planner ranges or People Also Ask to mark each question High, Some or Unknown.

8. **Group with Claude.** Paste the bank into Claude with the grouping prompt from Lesson 2. Copy the result into a `Topic Map` tab: Topic | Main question | Supporting questions | Main page URL (planned) | Priority.

9. **Check the groups.** Merge any two topics that would need the same page. Split any topic whose main page would try to answer two different jobs.

10. **Pick a first topic.** Set priority 1 on the topic with the most purchase-stage questions and some demand. That's the page Module 2 builds first.

## Done when

- [ ] Your `Question Bank` has at least 40 questions, each with a source
- [ ] Every question has a stage and a demand mark
- [ ] Your `Topic Map` has 5 to 8 topics, each with one main question and page
- [ ] One topic is marked priority 1

:::coached
**For 1-on-1 coaching clients.** Map your whole market.

1. Grow the bank to 80 questions, with at least 20 at the comparison stage ("X vs Y", "best X for Y").
2. For your top three topics, search each main question in Google, ChatGPT and Perplexity, and note who answers it today.
3. Draft a 12-week publishing order for your topics.

**Bring to your session:** your question bank, topic map, the competitor notes and your publishing order.

**Your coach will:**
- Check that your topics match how your buyers decide
- Spot topics where a competitor is weak and you can win fast
- Agree your first three pages and their order
:::
