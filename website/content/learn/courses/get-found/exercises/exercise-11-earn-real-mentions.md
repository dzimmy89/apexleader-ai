# Exercise 11: Build a 90-Day Mention Plan and Send Three Pitches

:::key-concept
You'll find the outside sources assistants cite for your buyers' questions, build a 90-day mention plan across five channels, and send your first three pitches or listings. At the end you'll have a target list, a plan on your calendar and three real requests out.
:::

**Time:** 45 minutes · **You'll need:** ChatGPT, Gemini, Perplexity, Claude and Copilot (free tiers are fine), your question bank, your Business Details doc from Exercise 4, your data page from Exercise 10 · **Builds on:** Lesson 11

## Warm-up

:::checkpoint
Q: Who should send your pitches and community answers?
- [ ] An agent, on a schedule
- [ ] A freelancer posting from their own accounts
- [x] You, under your own name, after reading each one
Why: Mentions only work if they're honest and disclosed. Agents can draft; you check and send, so nothing goes out that you wouldn't stand behind.
:::

## Steps

1. **Pick five questions.** Choose your five most important buyer questions from the question bank, at least two at the comparison or purchase stage.

2. **Ask five assistants.** Run the source prompt from Lesson 11 in ChatGPT (search on), Gemini, Perplexity, Claude (web search on) and Copilot for each question. That's 25 answers.

3. **Log the sources.** Add a `Mention Sources` tab: Question | Assistant | Source cited | Type (review, directory, publication, podcast or video, community, competitor) | Link | Mentions us (Y/N).

4. **Group and rank.** Ask Claude to count how often each source and each type appears. Copy the top 10 sources into a `Targets` list.

5. **Build the plan.** Add a `Mention Plan` tab with 13 weeks and the five channels from Lesson 11. Put each target in a week, using the cadences from the lesson.

6. **Check your description.** Make sure every listing and pitch will use your one-version description from Exercise 4.

7. **Send request 1: a listing.** Apply to the directory or association listing that came up most.

8. **Send request 2: a pitch.** Use the pitch prompt from Lesson 11 for the newsletter or podcast that came up most, built around your data page or one specific idea. Read it, check the piece you mention is real, and send it yourself.

9. **Send request 3: your choice.** Another pitch, a review request round, or one helpful, disclosed community answer.

10. **Put it on the calendar.** Add the plan's weekly tasks to your calendar, and a reminder at week 13 to re-run the source hunt.

## Done when

- [ ] Your `Mention Sources` tab has 25 answers' worth of sources, grouped by type
- [ ] You have a `Targets` list of the 10 most-cited sources
- [ ] Your 13-week `Mention Plan` is on your calendar
- [ ] Three real requests are sent, by you

:::coached
**For 1-on-1 coaching clients.** Build relationships, not just mentions.

1. Pick the 3 people behind your top targets (an editor, a host, a community moderator) and read their last 5 pieces or episodes.
2. Draft a useful contribution for each that isn't a pitch: a correction, a data point, an answer.
3. Plan one piece of original data you could release next quarter for those three people.

**Bring to your session:** your sources tab, targets, plan, the three people and your draft contributions.

**Your coach will:**
- Help you choose the target with the best odds
- Sharpen your pitch around one specific
- Check your community plan against each community's rules
:::
