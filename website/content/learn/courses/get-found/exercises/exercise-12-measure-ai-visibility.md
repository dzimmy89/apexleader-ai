# Exercise 12: Build Your AI-Visibility Dashboard

:::key-concept
You'll turn your AI-visibility log into a fixed prompt panel, pull this month's numbers from Search Console, Bing and your analytics, and put all four on one dashboard. Then you'll decide whether to stay manual or try a paid tracker. At the end you'll have a monthly measurement routine and a clear record of what's working.
:::

**Time:** 45 minutes · **You'll need:** your AI Visibility tab from AI Marketing Exercise 2 (or your question bank), ChatGPT, Gemini, Perplexity, Claude and Copilot, Search Console, Bing Webmaster Tools, your analytics · **Builds on:** Lesson 12, and every exercise in this course

## Warm-up

:::checkpoint
Q: Last month ChatGPT cited you for a question; this month it didn't. What do you do?
- [ ] Rewrite the page straight away
- [x] Note it and wait: act only if the change holds next month and shows in another source
- [ ] Buy a tracker to find out why
Why: Answers vary from run to run. One missed citation is noise until it repeats and shows up elsewhere.
:::

## Steps

1. **Fix the panel.** Add a `Prompt Panel` tab with 10 to 20 questions from your question bank, across problem, comparison and purchase stages. Write each question exactly as you'll ask it. Don't change the wording later.

2. **Run it.** Ask each question in ChatGPT (search on), Gemini, Perplexity, Claude (web search on) and Copilot, each in a fresh session. Use the buyer prompt from Lesson 11.

3. **Record each answer.** One row per question per assistant: Date | Assistant | Question | Mentioned | Linked | Position | Who else was cited.

4. **Pull Google.** From Search Console's Generative AI performance report, note AI-feature impressions for the month and your top 5 pages. If your property doesn't have the report yet, note Performance clicks and impressions instead.

5. **Pull Bing.** From AI Performance, note total citations, the top cited pages and the top 5 grounding queries.

6. **Pull analytics.** Filter session source for `chatgpt.com`, `perplexity.ai`, `gemini.google.com`, `copilot.microsoft.com` and `claude.ai`. Note visits and any sign-ups or sales from each.

7. **Build the dashboard.** Add an `AI Visibility Dashboard` tab with the four blocks from Lesson 12. Ask Claude or Gemini in Sheets to build simple formulas that count mentions and links from the panel tab.

8. **Write what you changed.** In the notes box, list what you published, refreshed or earned this month, from Exercises 6 to 11.

9. **Decide on a tracker.** Time how long the panel took. If it fits in an hour, stay manual. If not, pick the cheapest tracker from Lesson 12 that covers the assistants your buyers use and start a trial. Keep five panel questions manual to check it.

10. **Hand it to your agent.** Add the dashboard numbers to your weekly SEO agent's brief from Exercise 8, monthly, and put the panel run in your monthly Search check.

## Done when

- [ ] Your `Prompt Panel` has 10 to 20 fixed questions and this month's answers from five assistants
- [ ] This month's Google AI impressions, Bing citations and AI referrals are recorded
- [ ] Your `AI Visibility Dashboard` shows all four, with a "what we changed" note
- [ ] You've decided to stay manual or trial a tracker, and written why

:::example
title: See an example result

Rolling Ledger's panel has 12 questions, so each run is 60 answers. In month one it was mentioned in 7 and linked in 4, all from Perplexity and ChatGPT, mostly for the comparison page. Analytics showed 11 visits from perplexity.ai and 6 from chatgpt.com, with 4 trial sign-ups. The panel took 40 minutes, so the founder stayed manual and wrote: "revisit at 20 questions or the spring launch."

:::coached
**For 1-on-1 coaching clients.** Tie visibility to revenue.

1. Run the panel twice this month, a week apart, and note how many answers changed between runs.
2. Add one revenue number per assistant: trials, calls booked or sales from AI referrals.
3. Write a one-page plan for the next 90 days: which pages, mentions and refreshes you'll do, and which dashboard number each should move.

**Bring to your session:** both panel runs, your dashboard and your 90-day plan.

**Your coach will:**
- Help you read signal from noise in your panel
- Check your tracker decision against your budget
- Agree the three numbers you'll report in your Capstone scorecard
:::
