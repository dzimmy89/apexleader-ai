# Exercise 5: Brief Your Priority 1 Page

:::key-concept
You'll collect real search data for your priority 1 topic, have Claude find the gap, and write a full brief. At the end you'll have a brief with every fact filled in, ready for Exercise 6 to turn into a page.
:::

**Time:** 40 minutes · **You'll need:** your topic map from Exercise 2, Claude (Research mode if you have it), Search Console, optionally an SEO data tool connected through MCP · **Builds on:** Lesson 5

## Warm-up

:::checkpoint
Q: What is the "gap" in a brief?
- [ ] The word count your page needs to beat competitors
- [x] What the top results miss and your experience can add
- [ ] The keywords competitors use that you don't
Why: The gap is the reason your page deserves to rank and be cited. It comes from what's missing, not from length or keywords.
:::

## Steps

1. **Pick the question.** Take the main question from your priority 1 topic.

2. **Get the top 10.** Search it in a private window, or have your MCP data tool return the results page. Save the titles and links.

3. **Read the top pages.** Ask Claude Research or your builder agent to open each page and list its H2 headings in a table. Check two pages yourself.

4. **Add questions and queries.** Copy the People Also Ask questions, your Search Console queries containing a word from the topic, and your question bank rows for the topic.

5. **Find the gap.** Paste everything into Claude with the gap prompt from Lesson 5.

6. **Pick your angle.** Choose one or two of Claude's angles that you can really support. Write one sentence on why you, specifically, can.

7. **Write the brief.** Paste the brief prompt from Lesson 5. Save the result as a doc called `Brief: [topic]`.

8. **Fill the facts.** Answer every item in "Facts I must supply". Use numbers you can prove and examples you lived. Delete any section whose facts you can't supply.

9. **Check the outline.** Make sure the H1 is the main question and the opening answer is 50 words or fewer. Move anything listed under "Not this page" to your topic map.

## Done when

- [ ] Your brief doc has all eight sections
- [ ] The gap is one or two angles you can support, with a sentence on why you can
- [ ] Every item in "Facts I must supply" is filled in or its section is cut
- [ ] The opening answer is 50 words or fewer

:::example
title: See an example result

The Groomer Books brief for "What bookkeeping software should a dog groomer use?" found that all ten top pages listed general small-business apps with a paragraph each. The gap: a comparison of how each app handles cash tips, van mileage and quarterly tax estimates, plus a recommendation by type of groomer (salon, mobile, solo). The founder filled 11 facts, including how long month-end took her salon clients before and after switching, and cut one section on payroll she couldn't support.

:::coached
**For 1-on-1 coaching clients.** Brief your whole first cluster.

1. Write briefs for the main page and two supporting pages in your priority 1 topic.
2. Check each brief's gap against what ChatGPT and Perplexity say for the main question today.
3. List the internal links between the three pages.

**Bring to your session:** the three briefs, the AI-answer notes and your link plan.

**Your coach will:**
- Sharpen each gap so it's clearly yours
- Cut sections that repeat what every competitor says
- Check your facts list for claims you'll need proof of
:::
