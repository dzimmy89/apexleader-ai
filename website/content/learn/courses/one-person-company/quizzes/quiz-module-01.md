# Module 1 Quiz: The One-Person Company
Checks whether you can hire, equip and manage AI agents like staff.

---

## Question 1
You want help with your business but have not decided what to delegate. What should come before choosing any tool?

- A) Subscribe to every always-on agent and see which one sticks
- B) List your recurring work and sort it into agent alone, agent drafts and I approve, and only me
- C) Give one agent your main email password so it can learn your business
- D) Wait until the products leave preview

**Answer:** B

**Explanation:** Sorting the work tells you which job to hire for first and which tasks must stay with you.

---

## Question 2
Which description best matches an always-on agent such as Claude Duet, OpenAI dots or Grok Bot?

- A) A chat window that answers only when you type
- B) A browser extension that fills forms on your laptop while it is open
- C) An agent with its own cloud computer that keeps working, checks your apps and comes back to you for approvals
- D) A free tool that needs no account

**Answer:** C

**Explanation:** All three run in the cloud, keep working when your device is closed, and ask for approval on consequential actions.

## Question 3
You are in the US with a Claude Pro plan and want Claude Duet. What is true in October 2026?

- A) Duet is a research preview for Pro and Max, admitted from a waitlist, and its background checks count toward your Claude limits
- B) Duet is generally available to every Claude plan worldwide
- C) Duet is only for Team and Enterprise plans
- D) Duet costs a separate fee on top of Pro

**Answer:** A

**Explanation:** Anthropic's help center lists Pro and Max, US accounts, waitlist access, and shared usage limits during the preview.

---

## Question 4
You have about $100 a month, need a working website, and want one Bot that can log into a web app and repeat a weekly routine. Which stack fits?

- A) Four entry chat plans from different vendors
- B) ChatGPT Pro alone without checking price or region
- C) No paid tools until revenue arrives
- D) One main entry plan plus Cursor Premium, which includes Grok Bot

**Answer:** D

**Explanation:** Cursor covers the website and includes Grok Bot for the routine, while duplicate chat plans pay twice for the same job.

---

## Question 5
A Grok Bot handling your social posts asks you to sign the shared computer into your bank. What is the right call?

- A) Approve it with Always allow so it never asks again
- B) Deny it, because all your Bots share one computer and agents never touch money
- C) Paste the bank password into chat
- D) Create a second Bot so the bank login stays separate

**Answer:** B

**Explanation:** Grok Bots share one cloud computer and its logins, so separate Bots are not a security boundary, and money stays with you.

---

## Question 6
Your dot drafted ten client follow-ups. You told it "draft follow-ups" earlier. Is it allowed to send them?

- A) Yes, drafting implies sending
- B) Yes, if the drafts look good to the dot
- C) No, asking for drafts does not authorize sending, and client emails need your approval
- D) Only if you pause the dot first

**Answer:** C

**Explanation:** OpenAI's docs say drafts do not authorize sends, and anything that leaves the business needs a human click.

---

## Question 7
You pause your dot after it misbehaves. What still needs checking?

- A) Delegated tasks and scheduled runs, because Pause stops only the current main task and does not undo finished actions
- B) Nothing, Pause reverses all its work
- C) Your ChatGPT password, because Pause logs you out
- D) Only the dot's avatar

**Answer:** A

**Explanation:** Pause stops the main task only; you stop delegated work in Activity and recurring work in Scheduled, and completed actions stay done.

---

## Question 8
Your org chart shows Claude Duet and a dot both reading your inbox. What should you change?

- A) Nothing, two agents catch more
- B) Remove approval rules so they can work faster
- C) Connect a third agent to break ties
- D) Give the inbox to one agent and move the other to a different job

**Answer:** D

**Explanation:** One owner per job prevents conflicting drafts and wasted usage limits.
