# Lesson 2: Pick Your Stack at $20, $100 and $300 a Month

:::key-concept
You do not need every AI subscription. You need one main "office" where most of your work and memory lives, plus one or two specialists for jobs your main tool does badly. Pick by the jobs you sorted in Lesson 1, then by budget, and add an always-on agent only when you have a recurring job for it.
:::

## What you'll be able to do

- Describe what each major 2026 tool family is best at for a solo founder
- Choose a starter stack that fits under $20, $100 or $300 a month
- Know which plans include the always-on agents (Claude Duet, OpenAI dots, Grok Bot)
- Avoid paying twice for the same job

## Why this matters

Subscriptions creep. Many founders end up paying for four chatbots that all do the same thing, and none of them knows the business. One well-used main tool with your files, connectors and instructions beats five tools you open once a week. Budget tiers also force a useful question: which job is worth paying for first?

## The tool families in October 2026

Prices change often, so this lesson names plan tiers, not exact prices. Always confirm on the vendor's pricing page before you buy.

| Family | What you get | Strong at | Always-on agent |
|---|---|---|---|
| Claude (Anthropic) | Claude chat, Claude Cowork desktop agent, Claude Code, connectors | Writing, analysis, long documents, file work in granted folders | Claude Duet (research preview, Pro and Max, US waitlist) |
| ChatGPT and Codex (OpenAI) | ChatGPT, Codex desktop agent, ChatGPT Space, plugins | Broad all-rounder, images, Codex for code and computer use on Mac | OpenAI dots (ChatGPT Pro and Business Premium; Enterprise beta) |
| Cursor (SpaceX agreed to buy its parent in 2026) | Cursor 3 with parallel coding agents | Building and fixing your website or app | Grok Bot is included in paid Cursor plans |
| Grok (SpaceXAI) | Grok chat, SuperGrok plans | Fast research, X and real-time topics | Grok Bot is included with SuperGrok, SuperGrok Plus and SuperGrok Heavy |
| Gemini (Google) | Gemini app, Google AI plans | Founders who live in Gmail, Docs and Drive | Not covered in this course |
| GitHub | Free code hosting, Copilot | A safe home for your site's code and history | Not an always-on business agent |

Two facts worth knowing:

- **Grok Bot comes cheap.** As of October 2026, Grok Bot is included with SuperGrok, SuperGrok Plus and SuperGrok Heavy subscriptions, and with Cursor plans from Premium up (Premium, Super, Ultra, Teams and Enterprise, per Cursor's pricing page after its October 12, 2026 plan change). Bot usage resets weekly. It launched as a beta on August 11, 2026, and early reports quoted plan access inconsistently, so check the current docs.
- **Claude Duet costs nothing extra but is gated.** It is included with Claude Pro and Max during the research preview, but only for US accounts admitted from the waitlist, and its background checks use your normal Claude limits.

## Stack 1: Under $20 a month

One main office. Everything else free.

- **Pick one entry plan** (around the $20 line): Claude Pro, ChatGPT Plus, or Google's entry AI plan. Choose the one whose free version you already like.
- **Add free tools:** a free GitHub account to store your website code, and free tiers of the other chat tools for second opinions.
- **Always-on option:** if you are in the US, a Claude Pro plan lets you join the Claude Duet waitlist. If you would rather have a Bot that logs into web apps for you, an entry SuperGrok plan includes Grok Bot. Pick one main plan, not both, at this tier.

Good for: a founder validating an idea, writing offers, doing research and building a simple site.

## Stack 2: Around $100 a month

A stronger main office, plus one specialist.

- **Main office:** the entry Max tier of Claude, which gives more usage for Cowork, Claude Code and, once admitted, Duet's background checks. Or stay on an entry plan and spend the rest on specialists.
- **One specialist:** Cursor Premium if you are building a site or app (and you get Grok Bot with it), or ChatGPT Plus if you want Codex and image generation alongside Claude.
- **Free:** GitHub for code.

Good for: a founder with first customers who needs daily drafting, a working website, and one always-on agent watching the inbox or a routine.

## Stack 3: Around $300 a month

Two offices and a department of agents. Only spend this when the agents replace real hours.

Pick one of these shapes:

- **Claude-led:** a Max plan as the main office (Cowork, Code, Duet), plus Cursor Premium or a SuperGrok plan for Grok Bots, plus ChatGPT Plus for Codex and images.
- **OpenAI-led:** ChatGPT Pro, which includes your first dot, plus an entry Claude plan for writing, plus a SuperGrok plan or Cursor Premium for Grok Bots. Check the current ChatGPT Pro price first; it is the biggest line in this stack.
- **Grok-led:** SuperGrok Heavy or Cursor Ultra for heavy Bot and coding usage, plus one entry plan from Claude or ChatGPT.

Good for: a founder with steady revenue who wants three or four recurring jobs (inbox triage, bookkeeping prep, weekly reporting, site updates) running without them.

:::try-it
Write down your three most time-consuming recurring jobs from Lesson 1. Next to each, write which tool family from the table fits it best. If one family wins two or three jobs, that is your main office.
:::

## Rules for spending wisely

1. **One main office.** Put your instructions, files and connectors in one place first. Memory and context are what make an agent useful, and they do not transfer between vendors.
2. **Add a specialist only for a job you named.** "Cursor for the website" is a reason. "Everyone is talking about it" is not.
3. **Watch usage, not just price.** Duet's morning brief and check-ins count against Claude limits. Grok Bot has weekly usage with optional pay-as-you-go top-ups. Dots had extended limits for the first month after launch. Heavy agent use can hit limits before the month ends.
4. **Review every 90 days.** Cancel any plan you did not open in the last month.
5. **Regions and accounts matter.** Duet is US-only in the preview. Dots on ChatGPT Pro is not available in the EEA, Switzerland or the UK at launch. Check before you pay.

Here is a prompt to pressure-test your choice:

```text
I run [business] alone. My budget for AI tools is [$20 / $100 / $300] a month.
My top recurring jobs are: [job 1], [job 2], [job 3].
I'm considering this stack: [list plans].
For each job, tell me which tool in my stack does it and what is left uncovered.
Flag any two plans that overlap. Do not quote prices; tell me which vendor
pricing pages I should check.
```

:::warning
Business plans and personal plans have different data settings. If you handle client financial or health data, read each vendor's data controls before you connect client accounts, and prefer business plans where workspace content is not used for training by default.
:::

:::checkpoint
Q: You have $100 a month, you need a working website, and you want one agent that can log into a web app and repeat a weekly routine. Which stack fits best?
- [ ] Four different $20 chat plans so you can compare answers
- [x] One main entry plan plus Cursor Premium, which also includes Grok Bot
- [ ] ChatGPT Pro alone, without checking its price or your region
Why: Cursor covers the website and includes Grok Bot for the routine, leaving room for one main plan. Duplicate chat plans pay twice for the same job.
:::

:::takeaways
- Pick one main office and add specialists only for named jobs
- Grok Bot is included with SuperGrok and paid Cursor plans; Duet with Claude Pro and Max (US waitlist); the first dot with ChatGPT Pro and Business Premium
- Background agent work uses real usage limits, so check them, not only the price
- Confirm prices and regions on vendor pricing pages before you buy
:::
