# Module 1 Quiz: Run the Company for a Week
Checks whether you can run a weekly operating rhythm with Claude Duet and your department agents while keeping every approval in your hands.

---

## Question 1
How should your Grok Bots, Dots and builder agents get their updates into Claude Duet's morning brief?

- A) Duet logs into each Grok Bot and Dot and reads their memory
- B) Each agent writes its report to an app Duet is connected to, such as an email label, a shared folder or a Slack channel
- C) You copy every report into Duet by hand each morning
- D) Duet replaces the other agents, so they don't report

**Answer:** B

**Explanation:** Duet reads the apps connected to your Claude account, so a single reports place in one of those apps is how other agents report in.

---

## Question 2
Your Monday scorecard has 14 numbers and the session runs three hours. What is the best fix?

- A) Add more agents to read the scorecard for you
- B) Skip the decisions block
- C) Cut to five to seven numbers that each have an owner, a target and a red line
- D) Move the session to Friday

**Answer:** C

**Explanation:** A short scorecard where every number drives a decision is what keeps the operating session to about 90 minutes.

---

## Question 3
Duet drafted a follow-up email after a sales call and you approved the wording. What should you do next?

- A) Check recipient, subject, body and attachments, then approve the send itself
- B) Nothing, it has already sent
- C) Tell Duet to send all future follow-ups without asking
- D) Forward the draft to the Sales Bot to send

**Answer:** A

**Explanation:** Approving the wording isn't approving the send, and each message that leaves the business gets its own human click.

---

## Question 4
Duet's brief recommends approving a customer refund that the accounting agent has prepared. Who issues the refund?

- A) The accounting agent, since it prepared it
- B) Duet, since it recommended it
- C) Any agent, as long as it's under a set amount
- D) You, in the payment tool, if you agree

**Answer:** D

**Explanation:** Refunds move money, and agents never move money; they can recommend and prepare, but the payment is your click.

---

## Question 5
Your Marketing Bot had great results but posted one reply to a customer without approval. What happens in Friday's review?

- A) Give it a second channel as a reward
- B) Fix the boundary with instructions, an Ask first rule and its scheduler role, and hold off on new scope until it has clean weeks
- C) Ignore it since the results were good
- D) Delete the Bot immediately

**Answer:** B

**Explanation:** A boundary problem disqualifies scaling that week, and the fix belongs in instructions, rules and access before any new scope.

---

## Question 6
Which agent is the right one to retire or pause?

- A) The one that costs you more time in corrections and approvals than it saves, three weeks running
- B) The newest one
- C) The one that asks for the most approvals on public posts
- D) The one with the lowest plan cost

**Answer:** A

**Explanation:** An agent that's a net time loss for several weeks should be paused, signed out and stripped of access, with its job description saved for later.

---

## Question 7
During the monthly access audit you find your Grok Bot computer is still logged into a site from a finished project. What should you do?

- A) Leave it, since only one Bot used it
- B) Give the login to a new Bot so it isn't wasted
- C) Sign out of the site and remove the connector or access, because all Bots on the account share that computer
- D) Turn on Auto-review and keep the login

**Answer:** C

**Explanation:** Every Bot on a Grok Bot account shares one computer and its logins, so unused access is risk with no upside.

---

## Question 8
Claude Duet is new and still a research preview. Which habit protects you most from its known limits?

- A) Trust its urgency ranking without checking
- B) Use it only on weekends
- C) Turn off its approval prompts to save time
- D) Correct it in the thread when it misjudges urgency and log misses for the Friday review

**Answer:** D

**Explanation:** Anthropic says Duet may misread a thread or misjudge urgency, so steady correction and a weekly review keep the brief accurate.
