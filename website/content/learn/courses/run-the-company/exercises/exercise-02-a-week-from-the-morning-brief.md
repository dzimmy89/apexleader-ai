# Exercise 2: Run Five Days From the Morning Brief

:::key-concept
You'll run your company for one working week from the morning brief: triage each morning, take one themed deep look each day, and close each day with a check. At the end you'll have five triage logs, a Corrections doc and a clear picture of where your agents and your brief need work.
:::

**Time:** 60 minutes · **You'll need:** Claude Duet (or a scheduled Claude, ChatGPT or Gemini task as a stand-in), your reports place and scorecard from Exercise 1, your department agents · **Builds on:** Lesson 2

The 60 minutes is your setup plus the first morning. Then spend about 20 minutes each following weekday.

## Warm-up

:::checkpoint
Q: Duet drafted a follow-up email to a prospect and you approved the wording. What still has to happen before it sends?
- [ ] Nothing; approving the wording approves the send
- [x] Check the recipient, subject, body and attachments, then approve the send itself
- [ ] Ask the Sales Bot to send it instead
Why: Approving draft wording isn't the same as approving a send. Each send is its own approval, and you check where it's going and what's attached.
:::

## Steps

1. **Shape your brief.** In Duet's thread, paste the brief format from Lesson 2 and this week's three priorities from your Monday session. No Duet access yet? Set a scheduled task in Claude or ChatGPT (or ask your Grok Bot chief-of-staff routine) to read your reports place, email and calendar each morning and produce the same format. Keep it read-only.

2. **Turn on daily notes.** Ask each department agent to post a three-line daily note (done, next, needs you) to the reports place by 8:00 AM on weekdays.

3. **Create two docs.** `Triage Log` with columns: Date | Item | Move (approve, decide, delegate, ignore) | Minutes. `Corrections` with columns: Date | Agent | What was wrong | What I told it.

4. **Day 1 triage.** Read today's brief. Give every line a move and log it. Time yourself. Before any approval of a send, check the recipient, subject, body and attachments.

5. **Day 1 theme.** Do the day's themed block from the Lesson 2 table. Use at least one Duet request, for example:

   ```text
   Prep me for my next customer call: last email thread, notes in the Sales folder,
   invoice status. One page. Don't contact anyone.
   ```

6. **Close the day.** Ask:

   ```text
   What did I approve today, what's still waiting on me, and what will the agents
   do overnight? Flag anything that needs me before 10 AM tomorrow.
   ```

7. **Repeat for days 2 to 5.** Same triage, the day's theme, the close. Log every correction you give Duet or an agent.

8. **Friday summary.** Ask Duet (or your stand-in) to summarize your Triage Log: total minutes, how many of each move, and the three items that took longest. Save it for Lesson 3.

## Done when

- [ ] Five days of triage are logged, with a move and minutes for every item
- [ ] You did each day's themed block at least once
- [ ] Every send, payment or refund in the week was approved or done by you, by hand
- [ ] Your Corrections doc has every miss from Duet and the agents, with what you told them

:::coached
**For 1-on-1 coaching clients.** Make the week count on your real business:

1. Add a "time saved" estimate to each delegated item in your Triage Log.
2. Ask Duet for one forecast or dashboard during the week (for example, next month's revenue from your pipeline) and check its inputs against source data.
3. Note every moment you felt tempted to say "just send it from now on," and why.

**Bring to your session:** your Triage Log, Corrections doc, the Friday summary and the forecast or dashboard.

**Your coach will:**
- Find the triage items that should never reach you and fix their source
- Check the forecast's assumptions with you
- Turn your "just send it" moments into safer standing rules, or keep them human
:::
