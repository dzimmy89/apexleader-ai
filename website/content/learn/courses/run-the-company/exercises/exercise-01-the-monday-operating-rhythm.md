# Exercise 1: Run Your First Monday Operating Session

:::key-concept
You'll set up the reports place, get every department agent to file a weekly report, build your scorecard and run the 90-minute session. At the end you'll have a scorecard, a decision log and one written assignment per agent for the week ahead.
:::

**Time:** 40 minutes · **You'll need:** your department agents from the Run the Business courses (or the sample Groomer Books setup), Google Drive, email or Slack for the reports place, Claude Duet if you have access (any Claude, ChatGPT or Gemini chat otherwise) · **Builds on:** Lesson 1

## Warm-up

:::checkpoint
Q: Five agents each send you a report. Two include "needs your decision" items with no recommendation attached. What's the best fix?
- [ ] Make the decisions yourself without asking
- [x] Update the report format so every decision item must come with a recommendation, and send it to all agents
- [ ] Drop those two agents from the Monday session
Why: A fixed format is what keeps Monday to 90 minutes. Fix the template once and every future report improves.
:::

## Steps

1. **Pick your reports place.** Create a `Reports` folder in Google Drive, a reports email label, or a `#reports` Slack channel. Share it only with you and your agents' scoped logins. If you use Duet, make sure that app is connected to your Claude account.

2. **Send the report format.** Paste the weekly report template from Lesson 1 to each agent, with the numbers that department owns. Ask each to save it as a skill or routine that runs Mondays at 8:00 AM. Don't have all five agents yet? Create the missing reports yourself this week, or ask Claude to draft sample reports for Groomer Books:

   ```text
   Draft five sample weekly reports (marketing, sales, accounting, builder,
   research) for Groomer Books, a $149/month bookkeeping service for dog groomers
   with 18 customers. Use the report template below. Make the numbers realistic
   and include 2 decision items per report, each with a recommendation. Mark the
   whole thing SAMPLE DATA.
   ```

3. **Collect this week's reports.** Confirm each one landed in the reports place and follows the format.

4. **Build the scorecard.** In Duet (or your fallback chat), attach or point to the reports and use the scorecard prompt from Lesson 1. Check three numbers against their source links.

5. **Run the session.** Set a 90-minute timer. Follow the agenda: scorecard, decisions, three priorities, assignments, access check. Record every decision in a `Decision Log` doc: date | item | decision | owner.

6. **Write the assignments.** Write one assignment per agent using the Lesson 1 shape: what, by when, done looks like, approval point, not this week. Send each in that agent's own channel.

7. **Brief Duet.** Tell Duet (or note at the top of your scorecard) this week's three priorities and the standing rule: don't send, pay, sign or delete without asking.

8. **Book the rhythm.** Add a recurring Monday 9:00 to 10:30 AM calendar event with the agenda in the description, and a Friday afternoon review slot for Lesson 3.

## Done when

- [ ] Every agent's report (or a sample stand-in) is in one reports place, in one format
- [ ] Your scorecard has five to seven numbers, each with an owner, a target and a red line
- [ ] Your Decision Log has every decision item from this week, each answered
- [ ] Each agent has a written assignment with its approval point and "not this week" list

:::coached
**For 1-on-1 coaching clients.** Run the session live on your real business:

1. Use real reports from every agent, no samples.
2. Time each agenda block and note where you ran over.
3. After the session, list every approval you expect to give this week and estimate the time.

**Bring to your session:** your scorecard, Decision Log, the five assignments and your timing notes.

**Your coach will:**
- Review your scorecard and cut or replace any number that doesn't drive a decision
- Sit in on (or replay) your decisions block and challenge the slow ones
- Help you trim the approval load to under four hours a week
:::
