# Lesson 3: Checking AI Output

:::key-concept
Every AI answer that leaves your business gets a human check. A short, repeatable checklist (facts, tone, promises, privacy) catches most problems in under two minutes.
:::

## What you'll be able to do

- Run a four-point check on any AI draft.
- Decide which outputs need a second person to review.
- Explain to your team why "the AI wrote it" is never an excuse.

## Why this matters

Your customers do not care who drafted the email. If it has the wrong price or promises a date you cannot meet, it is your mistake.

## The four-point check

1. **Facts**: every name, number, date and price matches your source.
2. **Tone**: it sounds like your business, not like a brochure.
3. **Promises**: it does not commit you to anything you did not decide.
4. **Privacy**: it does not reveal one customer's details to another.

## When to get a second reviewer

Anything legal, financial or sent to many people at once gets a second pair of eyes. A one-line reply to a known customer usually does not.

:::warning
Never let an assistant send messages or make changes on its own until the same task has passed your check many times by hand.
:::

:::takeaways
- Check facts, tone, promises and privacy.
- Use a second reviewer for high-stakes or bulk messages.
- You own what you send, whoever drafted it.
:::
