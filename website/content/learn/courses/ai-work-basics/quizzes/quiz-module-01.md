# Module 1 Quiz: Working with AI Assistants
Check that you can pick good tasks, write clear prompts and review what comes back.

---

## Question 1
Which of these is an AI assistant most likely to get wrong if you do not give it the information?

- A) Turning your meeting notes into a checklist
- B) Your state's current sales tax rate
- C) Shortening a paragraph you wrote
- D) Explaining what a spreadsheet formula does

**Answer:** B

**Explanation:** A tax rate is a fact the assistant may not have or may have out of date. The other tasks work on material you give it.

---

## Question 2
Which prompt is most likely to give a usable first draft?

- A) "Write an email."
- B) "Write a really good follow-up email."
- C) "You are the owner of a landscaping company. Write a follow-up to a client who got a quote last week. Under 100 words, friendly, with the quote amount below. Here is one past email I liked."
- D) "Follow up with the client, make it professional."

**Answer:** C

**Explanation:** It gives a role, a goal, the material, a format and an example.

---

## Question 3
An AI draft looks good. What should happen before it goes to a customer?

- A) Nothing, the assistant has already checked it
- B) A quick check of facts, tone, promises and privacy
- C) Ask the assistant whether it is correct
- D) Send it and fix any problems later

**Answer:** B

**Explanation:** You own what you send. A short human check catches wrong facts, the wrong tone, promises you did not make and leaked details.
