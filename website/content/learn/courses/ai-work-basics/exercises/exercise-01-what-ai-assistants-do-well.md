# Exercise 1: Find Your First AI Task

:::key-concept
You'll list the repeated tasks in your week, score them for fit, and pick one to try with an assistant. By the end you have a first task with a clear way to judge the result.
:::

**Time:** 15 minutes · **You'll need:** your calendar from last week and a notes app · **Builds on:** Lesson 1

## Warm-up

:::checkpoint
Q: Which task is the best first fit for an AI assistant?
- [ ] Looking up a supplier's current prices
- [x] Summarizing the notes from your weekly team meeting
- [ ] Deciding which employee gets a raise
Why: The meeting notes are material you give it, and you can check the summary quickly. Prices are facts it may not have, and the raise is a judgment that stays with you.
:::

## Steps

1. Look through last week's calendar and inbox. Write down every task you did more than once.
2. Next to each, note the minutes it took and whether you could check the result in two minutes.
3. Cross out anything that depends on facts the assistant would not have.
4. Circle the task with the most minutes that is left.

## What to bring to your session

Your circled task, how long it takes today, and what a good result looks like.
