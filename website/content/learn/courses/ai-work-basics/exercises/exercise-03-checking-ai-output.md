# Exercise 3: Build Your Check

:::key-concept
You'll turn the four-point check into a short checklist for your own business and run it on three AI drafts.
:::

**Time:** 15 minutes · **You'll need:** three AI drafts from your saved prompt · **Builds on:** Lesson 3

## Warm-up

:::checkpoint
Q: An AI draft to a customer says "we'll have it to you by Friday". Nobody decided on Friday. Which check catches this?
- [ ] Tone
- [x] Promises
- [ ] Privacy
Why: The draft commits your business to a date nobody agreed to.
:::

## Steps

1. Copy the four checks into a note and add one line under each that is specific to your business (for example "prices match the current price list").
2. Run the checklist on three drafts. Mark every problem you find.
3. If a problem shows up twice, add a line to your prompt to prevent it.

## What to bring to your session

Your checklist and the problems it caught.
